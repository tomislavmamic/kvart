# Residential UPUs: the City already wants them, and the freeze is already on paper

*Updated 23 September 2026 with map 4.d of the April 2025 GUP draft and map 4.c of the GUP in force.*

A UPU (urbanistički plan uređenja) for residential Dračevac and another for residential Bilice is **legally doable, and its cost can be estimated**. The map check changes the picture on two points. First, **Split's own GUP draft already plans them.** Map 4.d of the April 2025 draft amendments to the GUP (generalni urbanistički plan), prepared for the repeat public consultation, lists 34 obligatory new UPUs. They include **"UPU Dračevac 2"** for the residential north-east of Dračevac and **"UPU Mostine"** and **"UPU Harakovac"** next to it. Most of that land is shaded as **urbana sanacija** (sanation of informally built areas). Second, **the building freeze neighbours fear is not something the initiative would introduce.** Map 4.c of the GUP in force has marked the same areas "obveza izrade UPU" since the 2008 amendments. The GUP's rules route new building there through that plan: SGGS 55/14, čl. 73 and 104–105. SGGS is the Službeni glasnik Grada Splita, the city's official gazette. The draft would keep the obligation and tighten it.

Bilice is a different case. About 62% of the kvart's area is already under **UPU Bilice II–Mostine (1998)**. According to one 2024 objection, that plan zones more than 100 family houses as a business zone. That count is the objector's, not verified. Three separate requests to change the zoning were refused in the 2024 consultation.

The proposal therefore stops being "please make us a plan" and becomes three concrete requests:
- **Dračevac:** finish the plan the City itself prescribes, and lift the freeze in the meantime.
- **Bilice:** replace the 1998 plan with a residential one.
- **Money:** fund both.

With measured areas, the cost is about **€50k–60k before VAT for Dračevac 2 (≈30 ha)** and **about €50k for a new Bilice plan (≈26 ha)**. That is roughly **€125k–140k with VAT for both**, or about €1,000–1,500/ha with a €50k minimum per plan.

## The freeze exists today on paper, and the draft would keep it

**GUP in force (SGGS 55/14):**
- **Čl. 105:** the GUP "utvrđuje obvezu izrade" plans for the areas on map 4.c. Until they exist, it allows permits only for a list of exceptions: parts of the street network, infrastructure and similar.
- **Čl. 104:** in the low-consolidated (niskokonsolidirana) areas, building goes through the plans in force or the prescribed plans. Direct application of the GUP is left for "područja za koja nije propisana izrada provedbenog dokumenta", areas where no plan is prescribed.
- **Rule 3.1 (čl. 73)** says the same for mixed-use M1 zones: new building, replacement and reconstruction go "uz izradu provedbenog dokumenta … ukoliko je … utvrđena obveza izrade", i.e. through the prescribed plan.

Read together, new houses in residential Dračevac formally wait for a UPU that has been required since 2008 and never started ([GUP SGGS 55/14](https://split.hr/strateski-dokumenti/prostorno-planska-dokumentacija/planovi-na-snazi/gis-podatci?EntryId=6130&Command=Core_Download)).

**Unverified:**
- Whether the City refuses permits there in practice. A separate paragraph on built parts of low-consolidated areas allows some works "kroz izradu propisanog provedbenog dokumenta … ili na drugi način utvrđen ovom Odlukom", which may leave a route open.
- Which urban rule (urbano pravilo) applies to each street.

**The April 2025 draft:**
- **Čl. 103(1):** building in neuređeno, preobrazba and sanacija areas is allowed only on the basis of a narrower plan.
- **Čl. 105(5):** until then, permits are allowed only for streets, small infrastructure, public buildings, small recreation areas and recycling yards. Houses are not on the list.
- **Čl. 108(1):** a house whose use conflicts with its zone may be maintained and reconstructed only within its existing footprint.
- **Čl. 103(4):** by contrast, a plan the GUP only recommends ("preporuka") leaves direct building under the GUP in place until it exists.

([ID GUP Odredbe, April 2025](https://split.hr/Portals/0/Dokumenti/Prostorno-planska/Izmjene%20i%20dopune%20Generalnog%20urbanisti%C4%8Dkog%20plana%20Splita%20za%20ponovnu%20javnu%20raspravu/1_%20Odredbe%20za%20provedbu.pdf))

The new national law is softer. Under NN 155/25 art. 106(3), an area waiting for a sanation UPU still allows reconstruction and like-for-like replacement ([NN 155/25](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2025_12_155_2315.html)).

**How the City has answered so far.** In the 2024 consultation a designer acting for the owners of three specific parcels (k.č. 1025, 1024/1 and 1024/2; the cadastral municipality is not named) asked to lift "nemogućnost donošenja akata o gradnji do donošenja UPU-a" (the ban on building permits until the UPU) for those parcels. The City refused. It replied that 1024/1 and 1024/2 are correctly shown as unserviced land and that 1025 lies in an urbana sanacija area (no. 208). This is one site-specific request, not a general one, and its location is not confirmed to be in Dračevac or Bilice. The county's environment department questioned **"33 nova UPU-a"**, noting that the areas are mostly built up and that such plans "često … ostaju mrtvo slovo na papiru" (often stay dead letters). The City replied that the new UPUs have "status sanacijskog plana ili plana urbane preobrazbe što je zakonom propisana obveza": sanation or transformation status is a legal obligation ([Izvješće o javnoj raspravi, 26 Mar 2025](https://split.hr/ukljuci-se/prostorno-planska-dokumentacija/izvjesca-o-javnim-raspravama/izvjesce-o-javnoj-raspravi-o-prijedlogu-izmjena-i-dopuna-generalnog-urbanistickog-plana-splita-i-strateskoj-studiji-o-utjecaju-na-okolis-izmjena-i-dopuna-generalnog-urbanistickog-plana-splita)).

This changes how the proposal can be put to neighbours. Nobody is asking to add a freeze. The freeze is already the City's rule, and the only ways out are to get the plan made or to get the obligation lifted.

## Bilice: the problem is a 1998 plan that zones houses as business

**UPU Bilice II–Mostine** (SGGS 12/98, 26.2 ha) aimed at "sanacija neplanski izgrađenih dijelova" (fixing up the parts built without a plan), but it keeps most of the area as a work zone ([UPU Bilice II–Mostine](https://split.hr/strateski-dokumenti/prostorno-planska-dokumentacija/planovi-na-snazi/gis-podatci?EntryId=3101)). Residents said so in the 2024 consultation:
- One objection says that Bilice II (Gradski kotar Mejaši) has **"preko 100 obiteljskih kuća"** in the K5 business zone, some lived in since before 1965. It asked for mixed use (M1). The City refused because rezoning "nije predmet izmjena i dopuna Plana", and added that "ozakonjenje građevina ne predstavlja kriterij za prenamjenu" (no. 150). A second M1 request for Bilice II was refused the same way (no. 128).
- A family of co-owners wrote that the UPU has held the area "u svojstvu taoca" (hostage) for 24 years. They had filed an initiative in March 2022 and never got even an acknowledgement. The City answered that such requests will be considered "u postupku donošenja novog GUP-a – kao 'plana nove generacije' i/ili izmjena i dopuna važećeg prostornog plana užeg područja", i.e. in the new-generation GUP and/or amendments to the narrower plan (no. 142).
- Residents of Ulica Bilice 2, with a 21-signature petition, asked for protective greenery (Z5) to become K5. Refused (nos. 47–48).

([Izvješće o javnoj raspravi, 26 Mar 2025](https://split.hr/ukljuci-se/prostorno-planska-dokumentacija/izvjesca-o-javnim-raspravama/izvjesce-o-javnoj-raspravi-o-prijedlogu-izmjena-i-dopuna-generalnog-urbanistickog-plana-splita-i-strateskoj-studiji-o-utjecaju-na-okolis-izmjena-i-dopuna-generalnog-urbanistickog-plana-splita))

**What this means for a Bilice proposal:**
- **No new freeze.** Building there already goes through the 1998 plan (draft čl. 103(5)).
- **Amending isn't possible, so it has to be a new plan.** Under NN 155/25 art. 238(1), plans adopted under earlier laws can no longer be amended except as new-generation plans. So "fix Bilice II–Mostine" in practice means **a new UPU for Bilice that replaces it**. That is exactly the City's own promised route.
- **The land use has to come first.** The GUP land use must allow housing there, because a UPU must follow the GUP (art. 85(3)). The rezoning request therefore belongs in the next GUP, with the UPU following it.

UPU Bilice sjever (SGGS 7/09) is the same story further north-west: about 40 existing houses zoned for production and business, with no housing allowed. The site's Bilice polygon barely touches it, so it is noted here, not proposed.

## What the City does elsewhere

Split has about 15 UPUs in force. Inside the GUP area the residential ones are few and old:
- Vrh Sućidra (SGGS 10/03)
- Mejaši–Dragovode (21/06)
- Stobreč (5/11)
- Šine–Vidovac (23/11). This is the clearest case of planning an already built neighbourhood: it keeps and expands the Kamen–Šine school and allows septic tanks until sewers arrive ([UPU Šine–Vidovac](https://split.hr/strateski-dokumenti/prostorno-planska-dokumentacija/planovi-na-snazi/gis-podatci?EntryId=3110)).

The nearest current precedents:
- **UPU Put Stinica/Put Supavla** (decision SGGS 23/23) covers an area "dijelom izgrađen pretežito bespravnom gradnjom" (partly built, mostly without permits). Its goals are the street network, utilities and public space. It is paid from the City budget ([Odluka SGGS 23/23](https://split.hr/Portals/0/adam/Contents/l9lHOskPX061RyqHuugxXQ/Link/Odluka%20o%20izradi%20Urbanisti%C4%8Dkog%20plana%20ure%C4%91enja%20podru%C4%8Dja%20sjeverozapadno%20od%20kri%C5%BEanja%20ulica%20Put%20Stinica%20i%20Put%20Supavla.pdf)). It is now listed among plans at public inspection ([split.hr, javni uvid](https://split.hr/ukljuci-se/prostorno-planska-dokumentacija/planovi-na-javnom-uvidu)).
- **Orišac:** in September 2026 the mayor announced a UPU for a densely built area with no organised streets ([Dalmatinski portal, 22 Sep 2026](https://dalmatinskiportal.hr/split-je-prostornoplanski-zapusten-duilovo-ide-putem-znjana-suta-otkrio-velike-projekte-koji-ce-mijenjati-grad)).

**Elsewhere in Croatia**, full UPU coverage exists only in small coastal towns:
- **Opatija:** 14 UPUs, mostly for whole settlements ([ZZPU PGŽ](https://zavod.pgz.hr/planovi_i_izvjesca/registar-prostornih-planova.ml?blank=true&grad=27)).
- **Mali Lošinj:** UPU-9 covers about 316 ha ([UPU-9](http://www.mali-losinj.hr/wp-content/uploads/2016/06/UPU-Mali-Losinj-Odredbe-za-provodjenje.pdf)).

The big cities plan houses through the GUP. Zagreb's 87 UPUs cover about **8.8% of its GUP area** ([Izvješće Grada Zagreba 2021–2024](https://informator.hr/files/832/8320bbe90f825582d990f677374a41cf6f3dcb4f.pdf)), and Rijeka applies most of its GUP directly ([Grad Rijeka](https://www.rijeka.hr/teme-za-gradane/stanovanje-i-gradnja/urbanisticko-planiranje/prostorni-planovi/generalni-urbanisticki-plan-grada-rijeke/)).

The closest warning is **Kaštela**. Of 35 prescribed UPUs it adopted 13, about 20 of them aimed at sanation and a better street network. In 2019 it judged their added value to be "nešto novih prometnica" (a few new roads): owners refused to give land and the city feared it could not buy it. Kaštela moved the street network into its GUP at 1:5,000 and **repealed 12 UPUs** ([Obrazloženje III. ID GUP Kaštela](https://kastela.hr/wp-content/uploads/datoteke/dokumenti/prostorni-planovi-grada/generalni-urbanisticki-plan-kastela---iii.-izmjene-i-dopune/tekstualni-dio/obrazlozenje-iii.-izmjena-i-dopuna-generalnog-urbanistickog-plana-kastela.pdf)). That is also the likely end state of an obligation that is never delivered: a freeze with no plan. The state institute's 2016–18 sanation pilots produced only drafts ([HZPR, Urbana sanacija](https://mpgi.gov.hr/UserDocsImages/Zavod/Publikacije/URBANA%20SANACIJA_zbornik_e-knjiga.pdf)).

## Cost, using the measured areas

No per-plan contract value for any Split UPU is published. The City's decisions to start plans say only that the plan will be financed "iz Proračuna Grada Splita", from the City budget. Recent public prices elsewhere cluster just under the €26,540 simple-procurement limit and are mostly amendments, so they are a floor:
- Zagreb estimated UPU Istočni kolodvor at €26,500 ([Plan nabave 2023](https://www.zagreb.hr/UserDocsImages/Nabava/PLAN%20NABAVE%202023.pdf)).
- Pula's EU recovery-fund (NPOO) grant came to about €20.7k per plan ([Grad Pula](https://www.pula.hr/hr/novosti/gradski-projekti/detail/27581/izrada-prostornih-planova-nove-generacije-putem-elektronickog-sustava-eplanovi/)).

The ceiling comes from the architects' chamber (HKA) method: about €860–2,040/ha for the plan alone, with survey (geodetic) base maps and studies contracted separately ([HKA Pravilnik 2013](https://arhitekti-hka.hr/files/file/komora/akti/pravilnici/Pravilnik-o-standardu-usluga-arhitekata-HKA-2013.pdf); [HKA 2012 examples](https://www.arhitekti-hka.hr/files/file/pdf/2012/URBANIZAM_CIJENE.pdf)). A usable planning figure is **€1,000–1,500 per ha all-in, and at least €50k per plan, plus 25% VAT**.

|---|---|---|---|
| UPU Dračevac 2 (the draft's boundary) | ≈30 ha | ≈€50k–60k (minimum applies) | ≈€62k–75k |
| Dračevac 2 + Harakovac as one plan | ≈80 ha | ≈€80k–120k | ≈€100k–150k |
| New UPU Bilice (replacing Bilice II–Mostine) | ≈26 ha | ≈€50k (minimum applies) | ≈€62k |
| **Minimal package: Dračevac 2 + Bilice** | ≈56 ha | **≈€100k–110k** | **≈€125k–140k** |

That is about 0.03% of Split's roughly €392M 2025 budget ([Dalmacija Danas](https://www.dalmacijadanas.hr/izglasan-rekordni-proracun-grada-splita-iznosi-vise-od-390-milijuna-eura/)). The real cost is the land and roads the plans will draw, which is what sank Kaštela. That needs its own line in the annual utilities programme (Program izgradnje komunalne infrastrukture). NN 155/25 art. 205 gives the argument: existing building areas must have basic utilities by 2031.

A landowner or other interested party may co-finance a UPU by contract with the City (art. 210). The contract can bind the City to a deadline for adopting the plan, but it cannot set the plan's content ([NN 155/25](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2025_12_155_2315.html)).

## Procedure, briefly

Under NN 155/25 ([NN 155/25](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2025_12_155_2315.html)):
- **Initiative:** anyone may initiate a plan (art. 112(1)). The City must analyse all initiatives received once a year and report to the mayor and the Gradsko vijeće (art. 112(3)).
- **Decision to start:** the **mayor** alone adopts the odluka o izradi (art. 114(2)). It sets the goals, the boundary and the participants (art. 115).
- **Expert debate (stručna rasprava):** closes only with two-thirds agreement of participants (art. 120).
- **Public consultation:** 30 days, with notice to the mjesni odbori, the neighbourhood councils (arts. 123–124).
- **County opinion** (art. 130), then **adoption by the Gradsko vijeće** (art. 132).

Realistic timing is **2.5–4 years** from decision to adoption. The UPU, the decision to start it and the GUP designation are separate levers:
- A UPU **can be started without being prescribed** by the GUP. The draft's čl. 103(7) says so explicitly.
- A UPU **must follow the GUP's land use** (art. 85(3)).
- The **GUP designation** is what creates the obligation and the freeze (art. 105(1) item 4 and art. 106(3); draft čl. 103).

## Recommended shape of the proposal

The recommendation follows the neighbours' position of no freeze. It turns the obligation that already exists from a freeze into a funded plan with a deadline.

1. **Dračevac, to the mayor.** Ask for an **odluka o izradi for UPU Dračevac 2**, using the draft's own name and boundary, optionally joined with Harakovac. Its stated goals should be the street network and turning areas, public spaces, and the conditions for legalised houses listed in draft čl. 106(2). Naš kvart and the mjesni odbor should be named as participants in the expert debate.
2. **Dračevac, into the 45-day GUP call.** Ask that, until the UPU is adopted, **residential Dračevac be treated as a recommended plan area ("preporuka", draft čl. 103(4))** rather than an obligatory sanation area. Direct building under the GUP rules for built parts would then continue in the meantime. If the City insists on keeping the obligation, the fallback is the softer national rule: transitional measures that at least allow reconstruction and replacement, as NN 155/25 art. 106(3) does. Either way, ask for a deadline for adopting the plan.
3. **Bilice, into the same GUP call.** Re-file the 2024 requests to rezone the housing inside Bilice II–Mostine from K5 to mixed use (M1). Cite the City's own reply that they will be considered in the new GUP or the narrower plan.
4. **Bilice, to the mayor.** Ask for a **new UPU for Bilice replacing UPU Bilice II–Mostine (12/98)**. Art. 238(1) means an old plan can only be changed this way.
5. **Budget.** Ask for about **€125k–140k with VAT** for the two plans. Add a separate land-and-roads line in the utilities programme, citing the art. 205 duty to service existing building areas by 2031.

**Fallback if the City prefers Kaštela's route:** the street network fixed in the GUP at 1:5,000, funded road designs for named streets, and the UPU obligation for residential Dračevac dropped. That also ends the freeze, but it gives up komasacija (land pooling) and exact public-use plots, which only a UPU provides.

## Conclusion

The map settles the question the first version left open. For Dračevac, the City already says a UPU is required, has said so since 2008, and would repeat it in the new GUP, so the freeze is the City's own rule. A proposal to deliver that plan, lift the freeze in the meantime and fund the streets is easier to defend to neighbours than a new request would have been. For Bilice, the obstacle is a 1998 plan that treats a neighbourhood of houses (more than a hundred, by one objector's count) as a business zone. The City has already said this belongs in the next GUP or in a new narrower plan. Both requests cost about €125k–140k with VAT in plans, and considerably more in land and roads. That second figure is where every earlier Croatian attempt failed.
