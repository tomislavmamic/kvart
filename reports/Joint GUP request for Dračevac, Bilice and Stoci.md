# Three small centres: a joint GUP request for Dračevac, Bilice II and Mostine–Stoci

*29 September 2026. For the City's call for GUP and PPUG initiatives, open **1 October – 16 November 2026** (javnipoziv.izmjenaplanova@split.hr). The call collects proposals for the **next** round of changes, not for the draft now being finished.*

**The idea in one paragraph.** Each neighbourhood gets a calm residential interior (M1). The interior starts from today's housing core and is extended to take in the homes around it. The main streets get a mixed frontage one plot deep (M2), with shops, services, offices and flats above. Commercial activity grows where the traffic already is, and business land behind the frontage stays as it is. Each neighbourhood gets a small centre at a key junction: a square, a public room or kindergarten, and ground-floor shops. Two of the three centres sit on land the City already owns. Interior streets become 30 km/h streets without through traffic.

"Houses" are buildings the City's cadastre lists as residential. They are not households.

## What each side gets

**Neighbourhoods:**
- About **450 registered houses** can be extended, rebuilt and added to under ordinary GUP rules.
- Today the draft keeps them in zones that freeze or block that:
  - K5 business-and-housing zones, where a house needs a UPU first (draft čl. 13(8));
  - I, K and Z5 zones, where a house can't be enlarged (draft čl. 108(1)).
- Vacant plots between the houses become building land for homes.
- Shops and services within 150 m of home, a public space, and quiet interior streets.

**The City:**
- **86% of the belt's business land (138 of 161 ha) stays untouched.** Another 11.6 ha becomes mixed frontage, where business can be denser and visible from the main street.
- The request delivers the draft's own goals for the eastern periphery (čl. 73(1)):
  - "formiranje uličnih poteza … uz značajne gradske ulice" (building street frontages along significant city streets);
  - "osiguranje prostora za … javne prostore i prateće sadržaje koji će povećati urbanitet naselja" (land for public spaces and services);
  - letting business work "uz minimiziranje negativnih utjecaja na stambene sadržaje" (while limiting its effect on housing).
- It follows through on what the City has already said:
  - the #142 answer that Bilice II "will be considered in the new GUP … after a traffic study";
  - the 2024 statement that the next GUP needs an analysis of which business zones can become mixed use.
- Centres on land it already owns: about **1.1 ha** of City parcels within 150 m of the Dračevac centre and **1.4 ha** at Bilice II. Those two centres don't have to start with expropriation.
- **Fewer and smaller UPUs.** The draft requires three large sanation UPUs here:
  - Dračevac 2: 30 ha;
  - Mostine: 52 ha;
  - Harakovac: 50 ha.

  The 1998 Bilice II–Mostine plan must also be replaced by 2033. Split hasn't adopted a residential UPU inside the GUP since 2011. With this request, the built parts would be handled directly by the GUP, and only the three centres would need a small plan or an architectural competition, the method the mayor already uses for Kopilica and Duilovo.

## Dračevac

![Dračevac: core extended south and north, frontage on the southern and eastern streets, centre on City land](Joint%20GUP%20request%20-%20map%203%20Dracevac.png)

**Today:**
- Residential Dračevac is K5, inside "UPU Dračevac 2", a plan required since 2008 and never started.
- No new house can be built until that UPU exists.
- The homes just south and north of the core are in a K zone and can't be enlarged.

**Proposed:**
- **K5 → M1** for the core, built directly under GUP rules.
- **M1 extended** to the 18 homes just south and north of the core, plus the vacant plots between the core and the southern main street (5 ha in all).
- **M2 frontage** on both sides of the southern street and along the eastern road (6.2 ha).
- A **centre on City plot 419/1** (0.4 ha, sports pitches, already marked R2 in the draft). It sits between the homes and the jobs of the Dračevac work zone.
- The lane through the core becomes a calm street. Through traffic stays on the southern and eastern roads.

**What changes for the better:** building resumes now rather than after a UPU with no date. The neighbours outside the core are included, there's land for new homes, and shops and a public space come within walking distance.

## Bilice II

![Bilice II: K5 core plus the Z5 houses, frontage on the north–south street, centre on City land at the pitch](Joint%20GUP%20request%20-%20map%204%20Bilice%20II.png)

**Today:** 119 houses sit in K5 under the 1998 UPU Bilice II–Mostine, and 94 more sit in Z5 protective green.

**Proposed:**
- **K5 → M1**, plus **M1 for the built part of the Z5 strip**. This is what the 21-signature petition (#47) and #48, #128, #142 and #150 asked for, now as one area.
- **M2** along the north–south street.
- The **Z5 strip along the Mostine road stays green** and can become the neighbourhood's park.
- A **centre on the City land around the NK Galaxy pitch** (1.4 ha within 150 m), at the seam between Bilice II and the work zone.
- The new plan replaces the 1998 UPU, which the new law requires by 2033 anyway (NN 155/25, čl. 238(2)).

## Mostine–Stoci

![Mostine–Stoci: K5 core, Stoci house plots joined to the Mejaši M1, frontage on the main streets, centre at the Stoci junction](Joint%20GUP%20request%20-%20map%205%20Mostine-Stoci.png)

**Today:**
- Mostine is K5.
- Stoci is an industrial (I) zone that owners subdivided into 500–600 m² plots before 2006: 118 of its 140 parcels are under 1,500 m².
- Every Stoci request of 2024–25 was refused.

**Proposed:**
- **K5 → M1** for Mostine.
- **M1 for the Stoci house plots**, joined to the existing M1 of Mejaši across the street. This is the "analysis of which zones can become mixed" the City's planner offered in 2024, already drawn.
- **M2** on the main streets around them.
- The industrial halls stay in I.
- A **centre at the Stoci junction**, where Mostine, Stoci and Mejaši meet. No public land was found there, so the City would need to secure a site through the centre's plan.

## How to deliver it: three options for the request

| | What we ask | Strongest point | Weakest point |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A (recommended)** | M1/M2 zoning. The GUP fixes street lines in the built M1, so permits are issued directly (NN 155/25, čl. 105(3), 106(4)). A small plan or competition for each centre only. | Ends the freeze; the fewest plans for the City | Most drawing work up front (street lines at 1:5,000) |
| **B** | Same zoning, but the City's UPUs stay, each with a deadline and interim rules allowing houses on existing plots along existing streets | Closest to the City's own method | The freeze lasts until each UPU is adopted |
| **C (fallback)** | No rezoning. Rules letting existing houses in K5, K, I and Z5 be extended and replaced, and the three centres as public projects | Least contested | Leaves the zoning wrong |

**How this differs from what was filed before:**
- The 18 submissions of 2024–25 were parcel by parcel and rested on legalisation. They were filed in a procedure that excluded rezoning, and all were refused (map 1 below).
- This request covers whole areas and rests on planning criteria.
- It is filed in the cycle meant for it.
- It gives the City something back: business frontage, centres on its own land, and fewer plans to deliver.

**How to file:**
- One petition across the three neighbourhoods: about 450 houses.
- Ask **Gradski kotar Mejaši**, which covers Dračevac, Bilice II and Stoci, to adopt it by council conclusion and submit it, as GK Kocunar did in 2024.
- The call's form is built for one parcel. Describe the areas in the text field and attach these maps; note 7 of the form allows that. First confirm with andrea.maslov@split.hr that an area request without land-registry extracts is accepted.

## How this was built

**Sources:**
- The four City hearing reports, split into 503 items and filtered to the belt by place name and by parcel.
- Zoning from the GUP in force (SGGS 55/14), which the 2025 draft keeps unchanged in all of these zones (checked on sheet 1 of the April 2025 draft).
- The DGU cadastre (refreshed 24 Sep 2026); the City's cadastral buildings and 3D footprints; public-land parcels from `public/geo/analiza/javne-cestice.geojson`; OSM streets.

**How the areas were drawn:** they are generated by rules, not surveyed.
- **Core:** today's K5 zone.
- **Extension:**
  - parcels with a registered house, plus undeveloped plots under 1,500 m² between them;
  - only in zones next to a core or to existing M1;
  - without large halls;
  - outside the Dračevac work-zone DPU.
- **M2:** a 35 m band along the GUP's street space, on the neighbourhood and on the business land directly across from it. The expressway is excluded.
- **Centres:** placed by hand at a junction, on public land where it exists.

**Draft rules quoted:** čl. 13(8) (K5), čl. 73(1) (eastern periphery), čl. 108(1) (buildings contrary to zoning), from [Odredbe, April 2025](https://split.hr/Portals/0/Dokumenti/Prostorno-planska/Izmjene%20i%20dopune%20Generalnog%20urbanisti%C4%8Dkog%20plana%20Splita%20za%20ponovnu%20javnu%20raspravu/1_%20Odredbe%20za%20provedbu.pdf).
