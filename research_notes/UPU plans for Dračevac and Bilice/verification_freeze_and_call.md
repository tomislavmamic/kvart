# Verification: building freeze, the GUP initiatives call, and UPU Bilice sjever

Research date: 23 September 2026. Every fact is dated.
- **Verified** means I (or a sub-agent, noted where so) read it in the primary document or official map layer.
- **Inferred** means reasoning from verified material.
- NN 155/25 = Zakon o prostornom uređenju, in force 1 Jan 2026 ([NN 155/25](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2025_12_155_2315.html)).
- ZoG 155/25 = Zakon o gradnji ([NN 155/25-2316](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2025_12_155_2316.html)).
- "ID GUP draft" = the April 2025 *Prijedlog ID GUP-a za ponovnu javnu raspravu*.

Companion to the notes in `urbanisticki-plan-primjeri-a5d462/research_notes/UPU plans for Dračevac and Bilice/`. Those notes were read first and are not repeated here.

## Bottom line

The draft recommendation points in the right direction but rests on four wrong premises.

1. **The call is not open yet, and it is not for the current GUP amendments.**
   - On 22 Sep 2026 the City only *announced* a 45-day call "za buduće izmjene i dopune, koje će se provoditi uz transformaciju", i.e. the next, new-system GUP.
   - The current ID GUP (odluka SGGS 44/21) continues separately under the old law. It has already drawn its map 4.d.
2. **Residential Dračevac already has a building freeze.**
   - The GUP in force (map 4.c, 2008 sheet) puts it inside a UPU-obligation area. GUP čl. 104–105 allow no new housing there until the plan is adopted.
   - The ID GUP draft keeps the obligation for about 85% of the "UPU Dračevac 2" area, now as urbana sanacija plus neuređeno.
   - So "no freeze" is not the status quo. A UPU (or UPU-level detail in the GUP) is the way *out* of the freeze, not into it.
3. **There is no "preporuka" UPU in NN 155/25 or its pravilnik.**
   - The lawful way to avoid the art. 106(3) freeze is **art. 105(3) + 106(4)**: the GUP itself sets conditions "s detaljnošću propisanom za urbanistički plan uređenja", and then a UPU "nije obvezna".
   - Pravilnik NN 152/23 has a map legend item for exactly this: "Područje provedbe s detaljnošću urbanističkog plana uređenja".
   - Asking the City simply *not* to classify legalised areas as sanacija cuts against art. 77(5): "Mjere za urbanu sanaciju **propisuju se** za područja na kojima se pretežito nalaze zgrade ozakonjene…".
4. **The site's "Bilice" is not in UPU Bilice sjever.**
   - The site's Bilice is Bilice II, east of the D8. It lies inside the 1998 **UPU Bilice II–Mostine** (GK Mejaši), zoned K5/Z5, with **no preobrazba** on draft map 4.d.
   - The ~40 houses of **UPU Bilice sjever** lie west of the D8, along Bilice I and Put Mostina (GK Neslanovac). They are zoned I (proizvodna) in the GUP.
   - On draft 4.d, 18 of the 30 small buildings there sit on the "urbana preobrazba" colour.

A corrected recommendation and the Croatian wording to submit are at the end.

## Q4. Is a "preporuka" UPU a legal category? What freezes, and when?

### Takeaway
- **"Preporuka" does not exist** in NN 155/25 or in the Pravilnik o prostornim planovima NN 152/23, which is still in force. No new art. 79(3) pravilnik had been published as of 23 Sep 2026.
- The freeze in art. 106(3) is tied only to art. 106(2) pts 2–5. Pt 1 (neuređeno) is separately blocked by ZoG art. 73.
- A GUP can avoid both routes lawfully by setting conditions at UPU-level detail (art. 105(3), 106(4)). The pravilnik's map legend has a category for that.
- **A UPU started voluntarily under art. 106(1) freezes nothing** while it is prepared. Unlike the 2007 ZPUG, the law has no zabrana in the odluka o izradi. (The brief's attribution to NN 153/13 was wrong.)

### Cited findings (verified from the NN texts; sub-agent read, and I re-read arts. 77, 106, 112, 135 and 148)

The word "preporu*" appears in neither the law nor the pravilnik in a UPU context.

### Inferences
- **Can a new-generation GUP mark "UPU recommended" without art. 106(3)?**
  - Legally, 106(3) is triggered only by pts 2–5, so an area not classified under 106(2) is not frozen.
  - But once the GUP draws a UPU obuhvat "za koje se donose" UPU, art. 105(2) pt 1 arguably stops the GUP from supplying building conditions there, which leaves a gap.
  - The pravilnik has no legend item for a "recommended" UPU.
  - The safe formula is therefore not "preporuka". It is **"područje provedbe s detaljnošću UPU-a"** (105(3) + 106(4)). Alternatively, the GUP draws no UPU obuhvat at all and lets an odluka o izradi set one under art. 106(1), as the draft čl. 103(7) already allows.
- **Sanacija cannot simply be avoided.** Art. 77(5) requires sanacija measures where legalised buildings predominate, which describes residential Dračevac (121/140 small buildings on the draft's sanacija colour). Those measures can be written into the GUP itself; art. 77(5) says "svi uvjeti provedbe … prostornim planom".
- **Neuređeno is a fact, not a label.** It means no sewer or drainage and no road access (art. 19 pt 33, and the draft čl. 106(1)), and the draft takes it from the PPUG. The status can end in three ways:
  - the City builds basic infrastructure (art. 205(1): existing building areas must be equipped by 1 Jan 2031);
  - the GUP sets UPU-level detail (106(4) covers pt 1);
  - a UPU is adopted.

  For pt 1 areas, ZoG art. 73(1) pt 6 blocks new-building permits until one of these happens, so "neuređeno" is a freeze too.
- **A voluntary UPU is safe while it is being made.** Nothing in NN 155/25 lets the odluka o izradi freeze permits, and permits follow the plan in force on the application day.

## Q5. Repealing or replacing UPU Bilice sjever; which plan governs; current zoning

### Takeaway
- **Procedure.** A repeal (stavljanje izvan snage) runs as a mini plan procedure:
  1. The **gradonačelnik** adopts an odluka o izradi for the repeal.
  2. The gradonačelnik sets the prijedlog.
  3. **Javni uvid lasts 15–30 days**, with a javno izlaganje explaining the reasons.
  4. The county zavod gives its opinion.
  5. The **Gradsko vijeće** adopts.
- **Partial repeal** is not provided for. In substance it is an izmjena, which art. 238(1) permits only as a new-generation plan. The cleaner options are a full repeal, or repeal coordinated with the new GUP.
- **Automatic lapse.** UPU Bilice sjever **lapses automatically by 1 Jan 2033** (art. 238(2)).
- **Current zoning.** The GUP 2014 zones the Bilice sjever houses **I – proizvodna**, urbano pravilo 3.1.
- **Which plan governs.** If a new GUP rezoned the land but the UPU stayed in force, MPGI's view is that the wider (GUP) provision prevails in a conflict. A GUP clause like the draft čl. 103(5) ("gradnja … samo temeljem prostornog plana užeg područja") would keep the UPU in control, so any rezoning must be paired with a repeal.

### Cited findings (verified)
- **Procedure articles in NN 155/25:**
  - Art. 135(1): the plan-making rules "na odgovarajući se način primjenjuju na … njihovo stavljanje izvan snage".
  - Art. 114(2): the gradonačelnik adopts the odluka o izradi. Art. 115(1) covers "zasebno stavljanje izvan snage".
  - Art. 121(3): the gradonačelnik sets the prijedlog.
  - Art. 124(2): javni uvid of a "prijedlog stavljanja izvan snage prostornog plana najviše 30, a najmanje 15 dana"; art. 124(3) requires a javno izlaganje on the "razloga za stavljanje izvan snage".
  - Art. 130(1): opinion of the county zavod. Art. 132(4): the gradsko vijeće adopts.
  - Art. 135(2): "Izmjene i dopune prostornih planova različitog obuhvata i/ili razine mogu se izrađivati i donositi istodobno."
- **Transitional rules:**
  - Art. 237(5): old plans "mijenjaju se i dopunjavaju u skladu s razinama prostornih planova određenima ovim Zakonom".
  - Art. 238(1): quoted in Q1. Its wording covers izmjene i dopune, not stavljanje izvan snage.
  - Art. 238(2): old-law plans "prestaju važiti u roku od sedam godina od dana stupanja na snagu ovoga Zakona" (exception in 238(5) for plans made through ePlanovi).
  - Art. 236(2) governs only procedures started before 31 Dec 2023.
- **Hierarchy:**
  - Art. 85(3): "Prostorni plan užega područja mora biti usklađen s prostornim planom širega područja iste razine."

  - Art. 86(2)–(5): the Ministry orders alignment. The city has 30 days to issue an odluka and 180 days to align; otherwise the plan is suspended and the "plan više razine odnosno šireg područja" applies.
  - The brief's "art. 82(3)" is the wrong article. Art. 82(1) gives plans the force of "podzakonski propis"; the consistency rule is art. 85(3).
- **MPGI opinion of 17 Apr 2026** (KLASA 350-01/26-02/121; list at [MPGI upute](https://mpgi.gov.hr/pristup-informacijama-16/zakoni-i-ostali-propisi/upute-objasnjenja-i-misljenja-3987/zakon-o-prostornom-uredjenju/6807)): "iako Zakonom … nije izrijekom propisano … mišljenja smo da je … u slučaju protivnosti odredbe … prostornog plana lokalne razine užega područja … ispravno primijeniti odredbu, odnosno dio prostornog plana širega područja." This is an opinion, not binding law.
- **GUP 2014 čl. 106:** it provides for "usklađivanje … odnosno stavljanje izvan snage svih navedenih provedbenih dokumenata prostornog uređenja ili njihovih dijelova". This is old-law wording.

> – površine javne namjene (dječje igralište, park, prostor za dječji vrtić);
> – najveću visinu i gustoću gradnje te obvezni udio zelenila na građevnoj čestici;
> – uvjete rekonstrukcije i dogradnje ozakonjenih građevina.
>
> Ako Grad ocijeni da je urbanistički plan uređenja ipak potreban, predlažemo da ga gradonačelnik pokrene odlukom o izradi na temelju članka 106. stavka 1. Zakona, bez propisivanja obveze iz članka 106. stavka 2. Zakona u GUP-u, tako da se do njegova donošenja gradnja provodi neposredno na temelju GUP-a, te da se Gradski kotar Mejaši i udruga podnositeljica odlukom o izradi odrede kao sudionici stručne rasprave (članci 115. i 119. Zakona).
>
> **2. Neizgrađene čestice bez osnovne infrastrukture (Dračevac, Bilice II)**
> Predlažemo da se za neuređene dijelove građevinskog područja unutar stambenih dijelova Dračevca i Bilica u GUP-u također propišu uvjeti s detaljnošću UPU-a (članak 106. stavak 4. Zakona), te da se ulice tih područja uvrste u Program građenja komunalne infrastrukture radi opremanja osnovnom infrastrukturom u roku iz članka 205. stavka 1. Zakona (do 1. siječnja 2031.).
>
> **3. Bilice II (područje UPU-a Bilice II – Mostine)**
> Predlažemo da novi GUP zadrži namjenu K5 (poslovna namjena i stanovanje) za izgrađeni stambeni dio ulice Bilice II, da čestice s postojećim kućama koje su danas u namjeni Z5 (k.č. 197/1, 197/2, 198, 199, 200 k.o. Split i druge izgrađene čestice) prenamijeni u K5, te da izgrađeni dio ne označava kao neuređeni dio građevinskog područja ni kao područje urbane preobrazbe.
>
> **4. Obiteljske kuće u ulicama Bilice I i Put Mostina (obuhvat UPU-a Bilice sjever)**
> Predlažemo da novi GUP dio proizvodne zone (I) na kojemu se nalaze postojeće obiteljske kuće u ulicama Bilice I i Put Mostina prenamijeni u mješovitu, pretežito stambenu namjenu (M1) ili u poslovnu namjenu i stanovanje (K5), te da taj dio izuzme iz područja urbane preobrazbe „zona proizvodne namjene na području … Bilica". Isti je prijedlog Gradski kotar Neslanovac podnio 18. listopada 2024., a odbijen je samo zato što „nije u skladu s Odlukom o izradi" tadašnjih izmjena i dopuna GUP-a.
