# UPU procedure under current Croatian law (as of September 2026), and how residents can initiate one

Primary source for all article numbers below: **Zakon o prostornom uređenju, NN 155/2025** (published 23 Dec 2025, in force 1 Jan 2026), full text at https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2025_12_155_2315.html. I downloaded and read the official NN text directly; quotes are from it. Abbreviations: "new ZPU" = NN 155/25; "old ZPU" = NN 153/13, 65/17, 114/18, 39/19, 98/19, 67/23.

## 1. Which law governs, which version is in force, and the transitional rules

### Takeaway
A **new Zakon o prostornom uređenju (NN 155/25)** has been in force since **1 January 2026**. It fully repealed the old ZPU (NN 153/13 … 67/23). Any new UPU for Dračevac/Bilice would be made under the new law, entirely inside the ePlanovi system. Existing plans made under old laws stop being valid within 7 years (by 1 Jan 2033), and from 1 Jan 2026 they can only be amended by producing a "new-generation" plan. The implementing Pravilnik o prostornim planovima has not been replaced yet: the old one, NN 152/23, still applies in the meantime.

### Inferences
- Split's current GUP and PPUG were adopted under old laws, so under art. 238(2) they must be replaced by new-generation plans by about 1 Jan 2033. The ongoing ID PPUG and ID GUP (odluke in SG 44/21, see §6) continue under the old ZPU per art. 236(2), but are also subject to the 7-year cut-off. This gives the residents a strategic opening. Split must remake its GUP anyway, so the residents can push for (a) the new GUP to designate Dračevac/Bilice as an obligatory UPU area for "urbana sanacija/preobrazba", and/or (b) a UPU to be started directly under the new law.
- Any UPU started now must use the new-law procedure. Old-law guidance (odluka by gradsko vijeće, prethodna rasprava, etc.) is **out of date**. Many online guides and Split's own older documents still describe the old procedure.
- The new pravilnik on plan content was due about June 2026. I could not confirm it was adopted by Sept 2026 (see Gaps). If it is adopted during the procedure, details of UPU content could change.

### Gaps
- I could not verify whether the new Pravilnik o prostornim planovima (art. 79(3)) or the uredba on urbanistički projekt (art. 151(4), due within 12 months, i.e. by 1 Jan 2027) had been published by 23 Sept 2026. The last evidence (MPGI, 6 May 2026) says it had not been.
- I did not check whether NN 48/2026 changed the legalisation cut-off rules in any way specific to planning (e.g. infrastructure corridors). Only a summary was read.

## 2. What a UPU is for, when it is obligatory, and what it can do in a built-up area

### Takeaway
Under the new ZPU a UPU is the most detailed local plan. It is **explicitly available for already built-up areas** where the plan foresees "urbana sanacija" (bringing informal or illegally built "prostorni nered" into planned order) or "urbana preobrazba". That describes Dračevac/Bilice well. A UPU can fix street layouts and road cross-sections, public-use plots (parks, squares, schools), communal infrastructure, maximum building volumes, mandatory green shares and urbana komasacija areas. Caveat: until such a UPU is adopted, only reconstruction or like-for-like replacement is allowed in that area.

### Cited Findings
- **Art. 84(4):** local-level plans are the PPUG/PPUO, the GUP and the UPU. **Art. 50** (definitions, art. 19 pt. 50): plans are "podzakonski propis" (art. 82(1): they have the legal force of subordinate legislation) — [NN 155/25](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2025_12_155_2315.html)
- **Art. 106(1):** the UPU "detaljno određuje prostorni razvoj, funkcionalna rješenja i uvjete oblikovanja pojedinih prostornih cjelina unutar građevinskih područja koje nisu s dovoljnom detaljnošću obrađene u prostornim planovima šireg područja" — [NN 155/25](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2025_12_155_2315.html)
- **Art. 106(2), where a UPU is adopted ("donosi se za"):**
  1. parts of building areas not built and not equipped with basic infrastructure
  2. **"postojeće i izgrađene dijelove građevinskih područja za koje se planira urbana preobrazba i/ili urbana sanacija"**
  3. registered cultural-historic ensembles
  4. maritime domain
  5. areas where urbana komasacija is planned
  - Source: [NN 155/25](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2025_12_155_2315.html)
- **Art. 106(3):** until a UPU under pts. 2–5 is adopted, work there follows the transitional measures of the wider plan. These allow only reconstruction of existing buildings and new construction replacing a removed building on the same plot, "kojom se bitno ne mijenja namjena, izgled, veličina i utjecaj na okoliš" — [NN 155/25](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2025_12_155_2315.html)
- **Art. 106(4):** a UPU is not obligatory for areas under pts. 1–4 if the wider plan (PPUG/GUP) already sets conditions at UPU-level detail. Art. 103(3) and art. 105(3) allow the PPUG and GUP to do so, except for areas planned for urbana komasacija. **Art. 106(5):** a UPU cannot be made for just one intervention with one primary land use — [NN 155/25](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2025_12_155_2315.html)
- **Definitions (art. 19):**
  - pt. 60, **urbana preobrazba**: planning measures that "bitno mijenjaju obilježja izgrađenog dijela građevinskog područja promjenom urbane mreže javnih površina, namjene i oblikovanja građevina, i/ili rasporeda, oblika i veličine građevnih čestica".
  - pt. 61, **urbana sanacija**: measures by which "stanje prostornog nereda dovodi u funkcionalno i planski uređeno stanje odnosno poboljšava karakter izgrađenih područja … devastiranih nezakonitim građenjem ili na drugi način".
  - pt. 33, **osnovna infrastruktura**: wastewater drainage "prema mjesnim prilikama" and "prometna površina preko koje se osigurava pristup do građevne čestice".
  - pt. 40, **površina javne namjene**: streets, unclassified roads, footpaths, squares, playgrounds, parks, schools, kindergartens etc.
  - Source: [NN 155/25](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2025_12_155_2315.html)
- **Art. 77(5):** "Mjere za urbanu sanaciju propisuju se za područja na kojima se pretežito nalaze zgrade ozakonjene na temelju posebnog zakona". **Art. 77(6):** urbana preobrazba measures apply to built areas planned for transformation — [NN 155/25](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2025_12_155_2315.html)
- **Art. 107(2), what a UPU determines** (depending on the area):
  1. detailed division into spatial units by use
  2. **building plots planned for public-use surfaces**
  3. regulation line, building line, footprints, **maximum gabarits and volumes**
  4. **public road and infrastructure network incl. cross-sections**, public parking, garages, transit stops, pedestrian and bike paths, communal infrastructure
  5. green infrastructure, incl. **mandatory share of greenery on private plots**
  6. heritage and interpolation rules
  7. **areas for urbana preobrazba/sanacija** and how reconstruction and new building improve quality
  8. **areas for urbana komasacija, which may define plot boundaries**
  9. **phasing and dynamics of implementation**
  10. other detailed conditions
  - **Art. 107(4):** the UPU must contain provisions ensuring new buildings fit existing surrounding development. Designers must prove this with a special analysis (art. 107(5)).
  - **Art. 107(1):** the UPU prescribes the implementation conditions for *all* interventions in its area.
  - Source: [NN 155/25](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2025_12_155_2315.html)
- **Hierarchy:**
  - The PPUG sets the obuhvat of the GUP and of UPUs outside the GUP (art. 103(1) pt. 7–8), and guidelines for UPUs (art. 103(2) pt. 6).
  - The GUP sets the obuhvat of UPUs within it (art. 105(1) pt. 4), plus guidelines and transitional measures (art. 105(2) pt. 3).
  - A GUP is made for the central settlement of a "veliki grad" (art. 104(2)).
  - A narrower plan must conform to the wider one (art. 85(3)), **but it may set stricter quantitative and qualitative conditions or higher standards** without that counting as inconsistency (art. 85(5)).
  - Source: [NN 155/25](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2025_12_155_2315.html)
- **Urbana komasacija (arts. 136–138):**
  - It can be carried out only on the basis of an adopted UPU that fixes the building plots, or of an urbanistički projekt where the UPU set only the komasacija area (art. 136(2)).
  - Owners get plots by area or by value, reduced by the share taken for public purposes (art. 136(3)–(5)).
  - Cash compensation is possible only if the owner's land is below the minimum plot size *and* under 10% of the pool (art. 136(6)).
  - Built plots that already conform to the UPU are excluded unless changed for public interest (art. 137(2)–(4)).
  - **The procedure is an administrative procedure run by the Ministry** (art. 138(1)). It is started at the request of the Government, a county assembly, **the city council**, or **owners of at least 51% of the komasacija area** (art. 138(2)).
  - Costs come first from the komasacija fund, then from whoever requested it (art. 138(12)).
  - Pending komasacije under the 2007 law are stopped (art. 241(1)).
  - Source: [NN 155/25](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2025_12_155_2315.html)
- **Urbanistički projekt (arts. 151–152):** a separate, faster instrument. Among other uses, it applies to "područja na kojima se planira provesti urbana komasacija prema području obuhvata urbanističkog plana uređenja, a kojim nisu utvrđene građevne čestice" (art. 151(2) pt. 3). Also for public-investor affordable housing and public buildings. It has a javna rasprava of at least 30 days (art. 152(7)). Adopted by the representative body; it counts as a lokacijska dozvola (art. 152(12)) — [NN 155/25](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2025_12_155_2315.html)
- **Duty to equip building land (art. 205(1)):** local units must "komunalno opremiti" a building area within 5 years of it being set in a plan, and must **equip existing building areas within five years of the law entering into force** (i.e. by 1 Jan 2031). They must also adopt a Program izgradnje komunalne infrastrukture with a 3-year projection each year together with the budget. **Art. 206:** equipping building land is an obligation of the local unit and the infrastructure bodies — [NN 155/25](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2025_12_155_2315.html)
- **Art. 45(1):** existing building areas "ne mogu se širiti dok god unutar njih postoje površine koje nisu opremljene osnovnom infrastrukturom" — [NN 155/25](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2025_12_155_2315.html)
- **Transfer of land for public use (art. 213):** an owner of unbuilt land that a UPU designates for a public-use surface, or on which a plot cannot be formed, may transfer it to the city. Arts. 214–223 cover compensation and pre-emption — [NN 155/25](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2025_12_155_2315.html)
- **Parcellation (art. 202(1) pt. 4):** parcellation may follow a plan that "u grafičkom dijelu propisuje oblik i veličinu građevne čestice". So a UPU showing plots and public-road plots gives a direct basis for cadastral division — [NN 155/25](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2025_12_155_2315.html)

### Inferences
- In legal terms, Dračevac/Bilice fit **art. 106(2) pt. 2** (built-up areas needing "urbana sanacija": largely legalised and informal buildings, poor access). If parts are unequipped, they also fit **pt. 1**. The ask to the City should use this wording: a UPU "za urbanu sanaciju izgrađenog dijela građevinskog područja" under art. 106(2) pt. 2 and art. 77(5) of NN 155/25.
- A UPU can **reserve street corridors and turning areas as public-use plots, and fix road cross-sections**. That is the legal basis the City needs to expropriate or buy the land and build the roads. It can also cap volumes and density through max gabarits/volumes and mandatory greenery, and phase the work. On its own, though, a UPU does not build anything. Building needs the art. 205 Program izgradnje komunalne infrastrukture and budget money. The art. 205(1) duty to equip existing building areas by 2031 is a strong accompanying argument.
- Double-edged effect: once the PPUG/GUP names the area as needing a pt. 2 UPU, art. 106(3) restricts new building (other than reconstruction or replacement) **until the UPU is adopted**. Residents should expect a de facto partial building freeze. Owners of vacant plots may object.
- Legalisation and the UPU run in parallel. Legalisation (NN 48/2026, cut-off 21.6.2011) resolves individual buildings. The UPU's urbana sanacija measures (art. 77(5)) set the rules for future works around legalised buildings. Buildings built after 21.6.2011 cannot be legalised, and a UPU cannot legalise them either.
- Urbana komasacija is a powerful tool for creating a road grid where plots are irregular, but it is heavy. It is run by the Ministry, the UPU must fix plots, and it needs a city council request or 51% of owners by area. For a built-up area, targeted road reservations plus expropriation or art. 213 transfers are probably more realistic than full komasacija.

### Gaps
- The new ZPU contains no definition of "izgrađeni/neizgrađeni dio građevinskog područja" in art. 19 (I found none). The Pravilnik NN 152/23 may define it; not checked.
- I did not check whether Split's GUP/PPUG already applies "UPU-level detail" to Dračevac/Bilice (art. 106(4)). If it does, the City may argue that no UPU is needed. Other researchers covering Split's plans should confirm.
- I did not verify whether Dračevac/Bilice lie within the ZOP "prostor ograničenja" (1,000 m inland from the coastline). This decides whether Ministry suglasnost is needed (see §3).

## 3. Step-by-step procedure (new ZPU), with articles and time limits

### Takeaway
The new procedure runs:

initiative → **odluka o izradi by the gradonačelnik** → notice, and requests to public bodies → **stručna rasprava** (mandatory for a new plan; closes only with 2/3 of participants agreeing) → prijedlog set by the gradonačelnik → **javna rasprava** (announced ≥8 days ahead; javni uvid **30 days** for a new plan) → izvješće → konačni prijedlog → **county zavod mišljenje (30 days)** → Ministry suglasnost (only inside the coastal prostor ograničenja; 90 days) → **adoption by the Gradsko vijeće** → publication.

The legal minimum, excluding drafting time, is roughly 4–6 months. Realistically it takes 2–4+ years.

### Cited Findings (in procedural order)
0. **Actors.**
   - *Nositelj izrade* for a local plan is the city's "stručno upravno tijelo" (art. 108(2)). It may appoint a city zavod as coordinator (art. 108(3)). The coordinator cannot also be the drafter (art. 108(4)).
   - *Stručni izrađivač*: zavodi or licensed firms (art. 109(2)). A city zavod may draft a UPU "ako to zatraži gradonačelnik" (art. 29(2)).
   - The responsible lead must have at least 4 years' experience (art. 109(4)). The lead must warn the nositelj and the Ministry in writing about unlawful requests and stop work until the Ministry answers (art. 111).
   - Source: [NN 155/25](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2025_12_155_2315.html)
1. **Initiative (art. 112).**
   - "(1) Izradu prostornog plana lokalne … razine, kao i njegovih izmjena i dopuna **može inicirati svatko**."
   - (2) Public bodies must send proposals each year by the end of February.
   - (3) The nositelj *may* run a savjetovanje sa zainteresiranom javnošću, and "**dužno je jednom godišnje napraviti stručnu analizu zaprimljenih inicijativa**". It must inform the gradonačelnik *and* the representative body (gradsko vijeće) of the conclusions.
   - Source: [NN 155/25](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2025_12_155_2315.html)
2. **SPUO (art. 113).** Strategic environmental assessment applies to all plans, but it is not needed for a GUP/UPU "ako je ista provedena za prostorni plan uređenja grada". MPGI (27 Mar 2026) issued separate guidance on the SPUO obligation — [NN 155/25](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2025_12_155_2315.html); [MPGI upute](https://mpgi.gov.hr/pristup-informacijama-16/zakoni-i-ostali-propisi/upute-objasnjenja-i-misljenja-3987/zakon-o-prostornom-uredjenju/6807)
3. **Odluka o izradi (arts. 114–115).**
   - Plan-making starts when the odluka enters into force (114(1)).
   - **For a local-level plan it is adopted by the gradonačelnik** (114(2)). *Changed from the old ZPU*, where the representative body adopted it; the MPGI 12.02.2026 guidance highlights this.
   - Published in the official gazette, on the nositelj's website and in the information system (114(3)).
   - The whole procedure is run in **ePlanovi** (114(4)).
   - Content (115(1)): goals and programme starting points, obuhvat, **list of public bodies "te drugih sudionika korisnika prostora koji sudjeluju u izradi"**, plus other elements set by the pravilnik.
   - Source: [NN 155/25](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2025_12_155_2315.html); [MPGI 12.02.2026](https://mpgi.gov.hr/UserDocsImages/dokumenti/Propisi/Upute_misljenja/2026_02_12_postupak_izrade_PP_ePlanovi.pdf)
4. **Notice and requests (arts. 116–117).**
   - The public is informed via the nositelj's website, the Ministry's website and the information system (116(1)).
   - The odluka is sent to public bodies with a deadline **set in the odluka** for their requests (116(2)). The law sets no fixed number of days.
   - Requests must cite their legal basis (117(1)). They cannot change the odluka's goals (117(2)).
   - Silence means no requests (117(6)).
   - Source: [NN 155/25](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2025_12_155_2315.html)
5. **Stručna rasprava (arts. 118–120).**
   - One or more held during drafting of the nacrt prijedloga (118(1)). Not required for amendments that do not enlarge the building area or significantly change the solution (118(2)).
   - Participants: the drafter, public bodies, "**druge sudionike i korisnike prostora određene odlukom o izradi**", and SPUO committee members (119).
   - It ends with a zaključak that "**dvotrećinska većina sudionika stručne rasprave suglasna** sa stručnim rješenjem". Otherwise it is repeated and the solution corrected (120(2)–(3)).
   - Source: [NN 155/25](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2025_12_155_2315.html)
6. **Prijedlog for javna rasprava (art. 121).** Set by the **gradonačelnik**. It must include a **sažetak za javnost** — [NN 155/25](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2025_12_155_2315.html)
7. **Javna rasprava (arts. 122–125).**
   - "može sudjelovati svatko" (122(1)). It consists of javni uvid and one or more javna izlaganja (122(2)).
   - Announced in a daily newspaper and on the nositelj's and the Ministry's websites **at least 8 days before it starts** (123(1)–(2)).
   - A *posebna obavijest* goes via ePlanovi to public bodies, to other participants named in the odluka and "**mjesnim odborima odnosno gradskim četvrtima** za područje obuhvata … prijedloga urbanističkog plana uređenja" (123(3)).
   - **Javni uvid: 30 days for a new plan**; 15–30 days for amendments (30 if SPUO) (124(2)).
   - Minutes are kept of izlaganja (124(4)).
   - How to participate (125): inspect the plan, ask questions, give proposals in the minutes, submit written comments by the deadline. Late or illegible comments need not be considered (125(2)).
   - Source: [NN 155/25](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2025_12_155_2315.html)
8. **Public bodies' opinions (art. 126).** If a stručna rasprava was held, public bodies may comment only on substantial changes. Unreasoned or late opinions need not be taken into account — [NN 155/25](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2025_12_155_2315.html)
9. **Izvješće o javnoj raspravi (art. 127).**
   - The drafter and nositelj assess each comment. The report lists the comments not accepted *with reasons* and is published online and in the information system.
   - **Before sending the final proposal to the city council, the nositelj must send each participant written notice with the reasons for (partial) rejection** (127(5)).
   - Source: [NN 155/25](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2025_12_155_2315.html)
10. **Ponovna javna rasprava (art. 128).** Required if changes depart from the odluka's goals, move the building-area boundary, substantially change land use, or **affect ownership and acquired rights**. Javni uvid **15 days**; comments only on the changed parts; **at most three times**, after which a new odluka and a new procedure are required — [NN 155/25](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2025_12_155_2315.html)
11. **Konačni prijedlog (art. 129).** Set by the same body as the prijedlog, i.e. the gradonačelnik — [NN 155/25](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2025_12_155_2315.html)
12. **County zavod mišljenje (art. 130).** Mandatory before adopting a PPUG, GUP or UPU; it checks consistency with the county plan and the law. It is due **within 30 days** of the request. The county zavod supervises the whole procedure. Not needed if the county zavod is the drafter or coordinator — [NN 155/25](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2025_12_155_2315.html)
13. **Ministry suglasnost (art. 131).**
    - Required for a GUP, for a PPUG in ZOP, and for a "**urbanistički plan uređenja unutar prostora ograničenja**" (the coastal zone).
    - Ministry deadline: **90 days** (131(4)).
    - If there are defects, the city has 90 days to fix them, otherwise the procedure is stopped and the odluka lapses (131(3), (5)).
    - Council amendments require a fresh suglasnost (131(6)).
    - Source: [NN 155/25](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2025_12_155_2315.html)
14. **Adoption (art. 132(4)).** A local UPU is adopted by the **gradsko vijeće** — [NN 155/25](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2025_12_155_2315.html)
15. **Publication (arts. 133–134).** In the city's official gazette (Službeni glasnik Grada Splita), and in ePlanovi within 15 days. Annexes (odredbe, kartografski prikazi, obrazloženje) are published within 30 days of entry into force — [NN 155/25](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2025_12_155_2315.html)
16. **Inconsistency enforcement (art. 86).** If the Ministry finds plans inconsistent, it orders the city to start fixing them within 90 days. The city then has 30 days to adopt an odluka and 180 days to align. Otherwise the plan is suspended and referred to the Visoki upravni sud — [NN 155/25](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2025_12_155_2315.html)

### Inferences
- **Legal minimum duration** (my own sum of statutory minimums, assuming no delays and a ready draft):
  - public-body request window: typically ~30 days in practice (set by the odluka)
  - stručna rasprava: weeks
  - announcement: 8 days + javni uvid: 30 days
  - izvješće: weeks
  - zavod: up to 30 days
  - (Ministry: up to 90 days if in the prostor ograničenja)
  - council session
  - Total: about 4–6 months, or 7–9 with Ministry suglasnost. Drafting by the stručni izrađivač usually dominates. So does procurement of the drafter (javna nabava) and any ponovna javna rasprava.
- Compared with the old ZPU, the new law removes the formal "prethodna rasprava" and makes the stručna rasprava the central expert step. It has a 2/3-consent rule among participants, and those participants are chosen in the odluka o izradi.

### Gaps
- The new law sets no statutory deadline for public bodies' requests; the odluka sets it. The old-law practice of 30 days could not be confirmed as still standard under the new law.
- I could not read the 2 Feb 2026 MPGI guidance on annulling and repeating javna rasprava, or the 5 Mar 2026 guidance on the Pravilnik. They are scanned PDFs and I did not open them.

## 4. Who can initiate; any duty on the City to respond; Izvješće o stanju u prostoru; financing by interested persons

### Takeaway
**Anyone** may initiate a UPU (art. 112(1)): individual residents, an udruga, a gradski kotar/mjesni odbor council. The City's planning department must analyse all initiatives **once a year** and report its conclusions to the gradonačelnik and the Gradsko vijeće (art. 112(3)). The spatial-planning law gives no deadline for answering an individual initiative. Local-self-government law adds a stronger route: a proposal backed by 10% of the city's voters must be debated by the council and answered within 3 months. The Izvješće o stanju u prostoru is where the City must list plan-making priorities; a new one is due by 1 Jan 2029. An interested person (e.g. landowners) may co-finance a UPU by contract with the City. This does not buy the plan's content.

### Cited Findings
- Art. 112(1): "može inicirati svatko". Art. 112(3): the stručno upravno tijelo "dužno je jednom godišnje napraviti stručnu analizu zaprimljenih inicijativa … te o zaključcima iste obavijestiti … gradonačelnika … kao i predstavničko tijelo". It may also hold a savjetovanje sa zainteresiranom javnošću — [NN 155/25](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2025_12_155_2315.html)
- Art. 114(2): the **gradonačelnik** decides whether to adopt the odluka o izradi. There is no appeal right for initiators in the ZPU (none found) — [NN 155/25](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2025_12_155_2315.html)
- **Zakon o lokalnoj i područnoj (regionalnoj) samoupravi** (NN 33/01 … 144/20):
  - Citizens may propose to the predstavničko tijelo "donošenje općeg akta ili rješavanje određenog pitanja". The body **must discuss it if supported by at least 10% of the unit's voters and answer within three months**.
  - Predstavke i pritužbe must be answered within **30 days**.
  - Vijeće mjesnog odbora adopts the committee's programme of work.
  - zakon.hr gives these as arts. 25, 26 and 61.c (article numbers from zakon.hr's rendering; verify against NN 144/20 consolidated text) — [zakon.hr ZLPRS](https://www.zakon.hr/z/132/zakon-o-lokalnoj-i-podru%C4%8Dnoj-%28regionalnoj%29-samoupravi); [NN 144/20](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2020_12_144_2762.html)
- **Izvješće o stanju u prostoru (arts. 39–41, 240).**
  - Considered by the representative body for a period of no more than **6 years** (39(1)). Prepared by the city's planning department (39(2)), or by the city zavod (art. 29(1) pt. 3).
  - Must contain "**prioritete za izradu prostornih planova i opremanja građevinskog zemljišta s indikativnim rokovima**" (40(1)). It is the stručna podloga for local plans (40(3)).
  - Every city must produce one **within 3 years of 1 Jan 2026** (art. 240).
  - Source: [NN 155/25](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2025_12_155_2315.html)
- **Financing.**
  - Art. 87(2): funds for local plans come from the state and local budgets "te iz drugih izvora sukladno zakonu".
  - **Art. 210:** "Troškove ili dio troškova potrebnih za izradu urbanističkog plana uređenja ili za izgradnju osnovne infrastrukture može snositi vlasnik zemljišta kojem to uređenje koristi ili **druga zainteresirana osoba**" that concludes a *ugovor o financiranju uređenja građevinskog zemljišta* with the City. The contract must state the UPU's name, obuhvat and "osnovne smjernice za njegovu izradu značajne za obje stranke", the **City's obligation to adopt the UPU and the deadline**, the City's works and deadlines, and the amounts. The money becomes city budget revenue (210(3)). Public-procurement rules still apply (210(4)). The contract is published in the official gazette (210(5)).
  - **Art. 211:** if the UPU covers land owned by persons who **fully** finance it, they may choose and pay the drafter directly. The city department remains nositelj izrade.
  - **Art. 212:** a financier of communal infrastructure is exempt from komunalni doprinos up to the amount paid.
  - Source: [NN 155/25](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2025_12_155_2315.html)
- **Investor "iskaz interesa" (art. 208):**
  - Where a building area lacks basic infrastructure, an investor may submit a statement undertaking to finance the UPU, resolve property rights and build the basic infrastructure (208(1)–(2)).
  - **If the City does not adopt an obligatory UPU within 3 years**, or does not equip the area within 3 years after the UPU, the investor is deemed able to act as infrastructure investor. Location and building permits for that infrastructure are then sought **from the Ministry** (208(3)–(4), (8)). Critics called this the most controversial provision.
  - Source: [NN 155/25](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2025_12_155_2315.html); [index.hr](https://www.index.hr/vijesti/clanak/puno-se-govori-o-bacicevim-zakonima-sto-je-tocno-sporno-u-njima/2739864.aspx)
- **Art. 207(2)–(5):** landowners served by infrastructure may themselves be its investor, with city consent and a contract. The finished infrastructure is handed to the city, possibly offset against komunalni doprinos — [NN 155/25](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2025_12_155_2315.html)

### Inferences
- **What to file:** a written "inicijativa za izradu UPU-a" under art. 112(1) NN 155/25, addressed to the Gradonačelnik and the Upravni odjel (nositelj izrade), copied to the Gradsko vijeće and the Zavod za prostorno uređenje Grada Splita. It should ask for:
  - (a) inclusion in the annual art. 112(3) analysis
  - (b) adoption of an odluka o izradi under art. 114(2)
  - (c) listing of the residents' association and the gradski kotar/mjesni odbor councils as "sudionici korisnici prostora" in the odluka (art. 115(1)), so that they are invited to the stručna rasprava (art. 119(1)) and receive the posebna obavijest (art. 123(3))
  - (d) inclusion of the UPU and the area's komunalno opremanje among the priorities in the next Izvješće o stanju u prostoru (art. 40(1), due by 2029) and the annual Program izgradnje komunalne infrastrukture (art. 205(1))
  - Separately filing it as a "predstavka" triggers the 30-day answer duty under the ZLPRS.
- **Escalation:** a proposal backed by 10% of Split's voters (roughly 15–16,000 signatures, my rough estimate from Split's electorate; not verified) forces a council debate and an answer within 3 months. However, the gradonačelnik, not the council, adopts the odluka o izradi under the new law. At most the council can pass a zaključak urging the gradonačelnik. The council keeps real leverage through the budget and the final adoption.
- **Co-financing:** residents or landowners *can* pay part of the UPU cost via an art. 210 contract. The contract can oblige the City to adopt the plan by a deadline, but it cannot dictate the content, which still goes through stručna/javna rasprava and legal checks. A residents' initiative is more likely to press the City to budget the plan than to finance it. A risk is worth flagging: an art. 208 investor "iskaz interesa" could let a single developer drive the UPU and infrastructure agenda if the City is passive.

### Gaps
- I could not confirm the exact article numbers of ZLPRS arts. 25/26 against the official consolidated text. zakon.hr's rendering was used.
- The law does not say how the annual art. 112(3) analysis is published, or whether initiators are notified individually.

## 5. Public participation rights and how residents can influence content

### Takeaway
Residents have guaranteed rights:
- to be informed (art. 16)
- to have their gradski kotar/mjesni odbor formally notified (art. 123(3))
- to a 30-day javni uvid with public presentations
- to written comments, and a written, reasoned answer before the council votes (art. 127(5))
- to a repeat rasprava if changes affect ownership or acquired rights (art. 128)

The most influential stage is earlier: being named in the odluka o izradi as a participant in the **stručna rasprava**, which needs 2/3 of participants' agreement.

### Cited Findings
- **Art. 16:** "Javnost ima pravo sudjelovati u postupcima izrade i donošenja prostornih planova". Authorities must inform the public and "omogućiti i poticati njezino sudjelovanje". There is also a right of access to spatial data. Art. 88: plans must be publicly available throughout preparation and validity via the information system — [NN 155/25](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2025_12_155_2315.html)
- Arts. 122–128, as detailed in §3:
  - anyone may take part
  - at least 8 days' notice
  - 30-day javni uvid
  - one or more izlaganja with minutes
  - written comments by the deadline
  - a published report with reasons for rejections
  - individual written notice before council adoption
  - ponovna rasprava of 15 days, at most 3 times
  - Source: [NN 155/25](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2025_12_155_2315.html)
- Art. 123(3) pt. 3: the posebna obavijest goes to "mjesnim odborima odnosno gradskim četvrtima" in the UPU obuhvat — [NN 155/25](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2025_12_155_2315.html)
- Arts. 118–120: stručna rasprava participants are set by the odluka o izradi. The rasprava closes only when 2/3 of participants agree — [NN 155/25](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2025_12_155_2315.html)
- Art. 112(3): the City may hold a savjetovanje sa zainteresiranom javnošću even *before* deciding to start a plan — [NN 155/25](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2025_12_155_2315.html)
- Art. 131(6): if the council amends the final proposal, a fresh Ministry suglasnost is needed where one is required — [NN 155/25](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2025_12_155_2315.html)
- Critics argue the new law reduces local-community control. The odluka o izradi and the prijedlog are now set by the executive, not the council — [dnevnik.hr](https://dnevnik.hr/vijesti/hrvatska/burne-reakcije-na-novi-zakon-o-prostornom-uredjenju-arhitekti-upozoravaju-na-ozbiljne-posljedice---947206.html)

### Inferences
- Practical levers for residents, in order of impact:
  1. Get the odluka o izradi to state goals the residents want (street network, public spaces, sanacija, limits on density). Art. 117(2) says public-body requests cannot override the odluka's goals, and art. 128(1) pt. 1 forces a repeat rasprava if later changes depart from them.
  2. Get named as a stručna-rasprava participant.
  3. Submit a well-argued, map-based written comment in the 30-day uvid. Oral comments in the minutes count too.
  4. Use the art. 127(5) written answers to lobby councillors before adoption.
- Comments that change ownership or acquired rights trigger a 15-day repeat rasprava (art. 128(1) pt. 4). Road reservations over private land are exactly such changes, so affected neighbours will get a second say.

### Gaps
- Split's own practice was not researched: whether it holds early savjetovanja, whether it names civic groups in odluke o izradi, how it runs izlaganja.

## 6. Grad Split specifics

### Takeaway
In Split:
- The **Upravni odjel za urbanizam, prostorno uređenje i zaštitu okoliša** acts as nositelj izrade.
- The **Javna ustanova Zavod za prostorno uređenje Grada Splita** (Trg Gaje Bulata 6; operational since 2023) can coordinate or draft plans.
- The **Javna ustanova Zavod za prostorno uređenje Splitsko-dalmatinske županije** gives the art. 130 mišljenje.

Split's major plan amendments (ID PPUG, ID GUP) have been in progress since 2021. No UPU for the eastern neighbourhoods (Dračevac, Bilice etc.) is currently in preparation.

Existing zavodi continue and must align their founding acts within 6 months (art. 245) — [NN 155/25](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2025_12_155_2315.html)

  - mjesni odbori and gradski kotari are legal persons whose areas are set by council decision
  - an initiative to establish a local self-government unit can come from 10% of the area's voters or from organisations based there
  - citizens take part in preparing annual work programmes through mjesni odbori and gradski kotari

## 7. Typical real-world duration from odluka o izradi to adoption

### Takeaway
I found no systematic Croatian statistics. Split's own examples point to **about 3–5 years** for a UPU and more than 5 years for GUP/PPUG amendments. The often-cited "2–5 years" range is consistent with this but is not backed by an official source I could find.

### Cited Findings
- **UPU Slatine 1 (Split):**
  - plan registration number "4/09"
  - javna rasprava: javni uvid 15.03.–13.04.2011
  - ponovna javna rasprava: 14.11.–13.12.2012
  - adopted by the Gradsko vijeće: Službeni glasnik Grada Splita 32/13
  - document dated August 2013

### Inferences
- Realistic expectation for a Dračevac/Bilice UPU under the new law, if the City agrees in 2026–27:
  - about 6–12 months for the odluka, the budget line and procurement of the drafter
  - 1–2 years for analysis (surveys, geodetic base, legalisation status) and drafting with stručna rasprava
  - 6–12 months for javna rasprava, possibly a ponovna rasprava (likely here, because road reservations affect ownership), the zavod mišljenje and adoption
  - **Total: about 2.5–4 years**, i.e. adoption around 2029–2030.
- The new ePlanovi-only workflow is itself new. Early procedures may be slower until the system and the pravilnik settle.
- Building the roads after adoption depends on budget, the art. 205 Program, expropriation and permits. That adds years beyond the plan itself.
