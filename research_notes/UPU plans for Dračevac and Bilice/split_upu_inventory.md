# UPUs in the City of Split: inventory, and what covers Dračevac and Bilice

Research date: 23 September 2026. Every fact is dated. "Verified" means I read it in a primary document, usually split.hr or the Službeni glasnik Grada Splita (SGGS). Items marked *(listing only)* come from the title in split.hr's plan library, and I did not open the plan itself.

Method: I crawled the "Planovi na snazi" (plans in force) document library on split.hr, which lists UPU, DPU and PUP folders, and read the "Planovi u izradi" (plans in preparation) and "Arhiva planova" (archived plans) pages. I downloaded and text-searched these documents: the GUP 2014 pročišćeni tekst (SGGS 55/14); the April 2025 ID GUP draft for ponovna javna rasprava (Odredbe and Obrazloženje); the odluke for UPU Bilice sjever, UPU Bilice II–Mostine, UPU Šine–Vidovac, DPU dijela područja Dračevac, UPU Put Stinica/Put Supavla and UPU Duilovo jug; and the obrazloženje of Bilice sjever. The main library URL is https://split.hr/strateski-dokumenti/prostorno-planska-dokumentacija/planovi-na-snazi/gis-podatci. Each folder opens as `?EntryId=<id>`. Folder 3094 holds the UPUs, 3095 the DPUs and 3093 the PUPs.

## Q1. Which UPUs (and DPUs) has Split adopted or started, roughly 2010–2026?

### Takeaway
Only about 15 UPUs are in force across the whole city (as of September 2026). Just four were adopted in 2010 or later inside the GUP area, and very few are plans for residential neighbourhoods: Šine–Vidovac (2011), Stobreč stambeno naselje (2011), Vrh Sućidra (2003), and partly Mejaši/Dragovode and Sirobuja. Since 2023 the City has started only a handful of UPUs: Put Stinica/Put Supavla (2023), Turska kula, Slatine 4 and Istočna obala (all 2025), and Orišac (announced September 2026). None has been adopted yet. None is in Dračevac or Bilice. After the 2013 Zakon o prostornom uređenju, Split largely stopped making DPUs. Several old DPUs have been, or are being, stavljeni izvan snage (repealed).

### Inferences
- The residents' premise that Split "commissions UPUs for its neighbourhoods" as routine is weak. In about 15 years (2011–2026) the City adopted no new UPU for a residential neighbourhood inside the GUP area. The last were Šine–Vidovac and Stobreč, both in 2011.
- The 2023–2026 wave (Supavla, Turska kula, Istočna obala, Duilovo, Orišac) is driven by city projects, strategic sites and traffic problems. Orišac and Supavla are the closest precedents to a "fix a messy built area" UPU.

## Q2. Has Split made UPUs for already built, informally developed residential areas? How did it go?

### Takeaway
Yes, but rarely. There are three documented cases: UPU Bilice II–Mostine (1998) explicitly aimed at "sanacija neplanski izgrađenih dijelova"; UPU Šine–Vidovac (2011) planned an existing neighbourhood; and UPU Put Stinica/Put Supavla (odluka 2023) covers an area "pretežito bespravnom gradnjom". In the opposite direction, UPU Bilice sjever (2009) zoned an area with about 40 mostly unpermitted houses as industrial/business with no housing allowed. The 2025 GUP draft would make "urbana sanacija" UPUs a legal category aimed precisely at the low-consolidated eastern neighbourhoods.

### Cited Findings
- UPU Bilice II–Mostine (1998), Odluka čl. 4: "Cilj izrade Plana je utvrđivanje sadržaja i prostornih mogućnosti građenja u granicama obuhvata, te uvjeta za sanaciju neplanski izgrađenih dijelova ovog područja." It covers 26.22 ha. The obrazloženje describes the area as a border zone with Solin, with "brojni konflikti" (many conflicts), whose "osnovno obilježje jest neuređenost" (main feature is lack of order). Several spatial units keep existing houses: unit 6 (2.44 ha) "dijelom izgrađena stambenim građevinama"; units 9, 10 and 12 keep 3–4 existing house groups inside work zones. The plan was based on a programme from the city Upravni odjel. — [UPU Bilice II–Mostine, SGGS 12/98, split.hr](https://split.hr/strateski-dokumenti/prostorno-planska-dokumentacija/planovi-na-snazi/gis-podatci?EntryId=3101)
- UPU Bilice sjever obrazloženje (2008/09): "najveći broj od oko 40 postojećih stambenih građevina sagrađen uglavnom bespravno, a većina od njih nema zadovoljavajući kolni pristup niti opskrbljenost ostalom … infrastrukturom" (most of the ~40 existing houses were built mostly without permits, and most lack proper road access and other infrastructure). Even so, the adopted odredbe say "Na području obuhvata Plana nije predviđena gradnja stambenih građevina", and in I and K zones "nije moguća gradnja stanova". Only "legalne građevine … u skladu s namjenom" (legal buildings consistent with the zoning) may be reconstructed. — [UPU Bilice sjever, SGGS 7/09](https://split.hr/strateski-dokumenti/prostorno-planska-dokumentacija/planovi-na-snazi/gis-podatci?EntryId=3108)
- UPU Put Stinica/Put Supavla, odluka o izradi (SGGS 23/23), čl. 4: the area is "urbanistički nedefiniran i dijelom izgrađen pretežito bespravnom gradnjom (objektima stambene, gospodarske i poslovne namjene) … s nerazvijenom i neuvjetnom sekundarnom uličnom mrežom" (undefined, partly built mostly without permits, with an undeveloped and substandard secondary street network). Its goals are road reconstruction, utilities, and public spaces to "povećati urbanitet". The City budget funds it, and it rests on a competition winner. Not adopted as of September 2026, about 3.5 years later. — [Odluka SGGS 23/23](https://split.hr/Portals/0/adam/Contents/l9lHOskPX061RyqHuugxXQ/Link/Odluka%20o%20izradi%20Urbanisti%C4%8Dkog%20plana%20ure%C4%91enja%20podru%C4%8Dja%20sjeverozapadno%20od%20kri%C5%BEanja%20ulica%20Put%20Stinica%20i%20Put%20Supavla.pdf); [Planovi u izradi](https://split.hr/ukljuci-se/prostorno-planska-dokumentacija/planovi-u-izradi)
- UPU Šine–Vidovac (2011) plans an existing built neighbourhood. It keeps and expands OŠ Kamen–Šine (1.31 ha plus 0.93 ha expansion, with a sports hall), keeps the kindergartens "Margaritela" and "Smokvica", keeps the MO Kamen premises, and allows septic tanks up to 10 ES (population equivalent) until sewers exist. — [UPU Šine–Vidovac, SGGS 23/11](https://split.hr/strateski-dokumenti/prostorno-planska-dokumentacija/planovi-na-snazi/gis-podatci?EntryId=3110)
- Orišac (September 2026): the Mayor wants a UPU to sort out traffic corridors in a heavily built area lacking organised roads. — [Dalmatinski portal, 22 Sep 2026](https://dalmatinskiportal.hr/split-je-prostornoplanski-zapusten-duilovo-ide-putem-znjana-suta-otkrio-velike-projekte-koji-ce-mijenjati-grad)
- The April 2025 ID GUP draft defines "urbana sanacija" areas as "većinom nisko konsolidirana područja na istočnom dijelu grada, na kojima je prisutna dugogodišnja nezakonita gradnja", where legalisation (ozakonjenje) made the illegal construction "trajne i velikim dijelom nepromjenjive činjenice u prostoru" (permanent and largely unchangeable), creating a "neplanski urbaniziran prostor kojem nedostaju … sustav javnih prometnih površina … te javni sadržaji". — [ID GUP Obrazloženje, April 2025](https://split.hr/Portals/0/Dokumenti/Prostorno-planska/Izmjene%20i%20dopune%20Generalnog%20urbanisti%C4%8Dkog%20plana%20Splita%20za%20ponovnu%20javnu%20raspravu/3_%20Obrazlozenje.pdf?ver=Gbe6mT0OKtlHf-a28MWVtQ%3D%3D)

### Inferences
- Bilice sjever is a cautionary precedent. A UPU in a partly residential, informally built area can re-zone it as non-residential and freeze the houses. Residents should make sure any initiative fixes the namjena (land use) first, through the GUP.
- How well Mostine (1998) worked in practice (roads built, houses legalised) is not documented in what I found.

### Gaps
- No evaluation or media coverage found on how Mostine or Šine–Vidovac were carried out, for example whether the roads were built.
- Whether the Supavla UPU has had a javna rasprava (public consultation) is unknown. As of September 2026 only the odluka is posted.

## Q3. What do the GUP and the 2024/2025 draft say about obligatory UPUs? Are Dračevac or Bilice listed?

### Takeaway
In the GUP currently in force (the 2014 pročišćeni tekst, SGGS 55/14), UPU and DPU obligations are set only graphically, on map 4.c "Obuhvat detaljnijih planova" (1:10,000). There is no text list, and the dinamika (timing) is left to individual odluke o izradi. The 2024/2025 ID GUP draft rewrites the system. A binding UPU obligation, with no permits for new buildings until the UPU exists, would apply only to (1) neuređeni neizgrađeni dijelovi (unserviced, unbuilt parts of the building zone), (2) urbana preobrazba areas and (3) urbana sanacija areas, mapped on the new map 4.d. Other UPUs become recommendations only. The text names "zone proizvodne namjene na području … Smokovika i Bilica" as urbana preobrazba. The residential urbana sanacija areas are described only generically ("istočni dio grada"), so whether residential Dračevac or Bilice are inside depends on map 4.d, which I could not read. **As of 30 October 2025 the draft had not been adopted, and no adoption was found up to September 2026.**

### Cited Findings
- GUP 2014, čl. 105: "Utvrđuje se obveza izrade urbanističkih i provedbenih dokumenata prostornog uređenja za obuhvate prema kartografskom prikazu br. 4.c … Obuhvat detaljnijih planova, u mjerilu 1:10.000." Čl. 109: "Dinamika izrade detaljnijih planova … utvrđivat će se odlukama o izradi". Čl. 104: in niskokonsolidirana (low-consolidated) areas, building is possible "temeljem ovog Plana, za područja za koja nije propisana izrada provedbenog dokumenta". — [GUP pročišćeni tekst SGGS 55/14](https://split.hr/strateski-dokumenti/prostorno-planska-dokumentacija/planovi-na-snazi/gis-podatci?EntryId=6130&Command=Core_Download)
- GUP 2014, čl. 88, "Gradski projekt Dračevac": about 14.0 ha, "prenamjena prostora u kompleks pretežito poslovnih, javnih i društvenih sadržaja", "isključuje se mogućnost stambene izgradnje" (housing excluded), up to 200,000 m² BRPN. It is exempt from the natječaj (competition) requirement. The GUP also names a "Mostine – Dračevac" bus terminus and K3 service zone. — [GUP SGGS 55/14](https://split.hr/strateski-dokumenti/prostorno-planska-dokumentacija/planovi-na-snazi/gis-podatci?EntryId=6130&Command=Core_Download)
- UPU Bilice sjever's boundary was set by GUP map "4.c – Područja … [obuhvat detaljnijih planova]" (the 2006/2008 GUP). This confirms the 4.c mechanism was used for Bilice. — [UPU Bilice sjever obrazloženje](https://split.hr/strateski-dokumenti/prostorno-planska-dokumentacija/planovi-na-snazi/gis-podatci?EntryId=3108)
- ID GUP Obrazloženje (April 2025) says that under the 2013 Zakon "na pojedinim područjima za koja je dosadašnjim GUP-om bila obvezna izrada … UPU-a … više se ne može propisati ta obveza, tako da gradnja postaje moguća izravno temeljem GUP-a" (the old obligation can no longer be imposed, so building becomes possible directly under the GUP). The GUP therefore adds stricter direct building rules: height, setbacks, green share, road widths. — [ID GUP Obrazloženje, April 2025](https://split.hr/Portals/0/Dokumenti/Prostorno-planska/Izmjene%20i%20dopune%20Generalnog%20urbanisti%C4%8Dkog%20plana%20Splita%20za%20ponovnu%20javnu%20raspravu/3_%20Obrazlozenje.pdf?ver=Gbe6mT0OKtlHf-a28MWVtQ%3D%3D)
- ID GUP Obrazloženje, point 2.1.1.4: the UPU obligation is "reducirana samo na neuređene dijelove građevinskog područja i na izgrađene dijelove … planirane za urbanu preobrazbu ili urbanu sanaciju". The GUP keeps "obveza preporuka izrade većeg broja UPU-a" as a planning recommendation. In obligation areas, no lokacijska or građevinska dozvola (location or building permit) for a new building may be issued before the UPU; in recommendation areas permits can still be issued. — [ID GUP Obrazloženje, April 2025](https://split.hr/Portals/0/Dokumenti/Prostorno-planska/Izmjene%20i%20dopune%20Generalnog%20urbanisti%C4%8Dkog%20plana%20Splita%20za%20ponovnu%20javnu%20raspravu/3_%20Obrazlozenje.pdf?ver=Gbe6mT0OKtlHf-a28MWVtQ%3D%3D)

## Q4. What exactly covers Dračevac and Bilice today? Which residential parts have no detailed plan?

### Takeaway
Four detailed plans touch Dračevac and Bilice, and none of them is a residential plan:
- UPU Bilice sjever (2009): 25.4 ha, industrial/business, housing prohibited.
- UPU Bilice II–Mostine (1998): 26.2 ha, mixed work zone plus retained houses, 28 years old.
- DPU radne zone Dračevac (2009, amended 2018–2025): business zone.
- DPU dijela područja Dračevac (2004): a single 0.46 ha industrial plot.

On top of these, the GUP's Gradski projekt Dračevac (about 14 ha) excludes housing. Residential streets outside these boundaries are governed only by the GUP (SGGS 55/14) through direct application.

### Cited Findings
- UPU Bilice sjever (adopted 10 Mar 2009, SGGS 7/09):
  - Total area 25.4 ha.
  - Uses: production (I), business (K), protective greenery (Z5). "Na području obuhvata Plana nije predviđena gradnja stambenih građevina" (no residential building planned) and no public/social buildings.
  - Stated goals: "sanacija, uređivanje i urbana obnova djelomično izgrađenog prostora jedne namjene" (sanitation, ordering and urban renewal of a partly built single-use area) plus new production.
  - Prepared by CPA d.o.o. Zagreb (lead Tomislav Dolečki). The obrazloženje shows the City contracted CPA in May 2007, which suggests City financing.

Križ" barracks (Ministry of Defence consent needed). — [UPU Bilice II–Mostine](https://split.hr/strateski-dokumenti/prostorno-planska-dokumentacija/planovi-na-snazi/gis-podatci?EntryId=3101)

## Q5. How long do Split UPUs take, and have any stalled or been abandoned?

### Takeaway
Adopted Split UPUs took about 2–3 years: Bilice sjever about 2 years (2007→2009) and Šine–Vidovac about 3 years (2008→2011). Recent ones are slower or stuck: Duilovo jug has had an odluka since 2016 with no plan after 10 years, and Put Supavla has had one since 2023 with no plan after 3.5 years. Odluke o izradi set very short nominal deadlines (about 5–6 months) that bear no relation to reality. Split also regularly repeals old plans.

### Cited Findings
- Šine–Vidovac: odluka o izradi SGGS 16/08 (2008) → adopted 8 Jul 2011 (SGGS 23/11), about 3 years. — [UPU Šine–Vidovac](https://split.hr/strateski-dokumenti/prostorno-planska-dokumentacija/planovi-na-snazi/gis-podatci?EntryId=3110)
- Bilice sjever: preparatory work started May 2007. The plan was "usporen" (slowed) because the City started GUP amendments right after work began. The prethodna rasprava (preliminary consultation) was on 13 May 2008, the draft for javna rasprava was set by the Gradsko poglavarstvo on 27 Jun 2008 (announced in Slobodna Dalmacija on 29 Jun 2008), and there was a ponovna (repeat) javna rasprava. Adopted 10 Mar 2009. About 22 months in total. — [UPU Bilice sjever obrazloženje](https://split.hr/strateski-dokumenti/prostorno-planska-dokumentacija/planovi-na-snazi/gis-podatci?EntryId=3108)
- Duilovo south: odluka 22 Mar 2016 (SGGS 16/16). Not adopted by September 2026; the competition is still pending. — [HZPR notice](https://mpgi.gov.hr/UserDocsImages//Zavod/Odluke_o_izradi/Splitsko_dalmatinska//16_04_25_UPU_juzni_dio_Duilovo.pdf); [Dalmatinski portal, 22 Sep 2026](https://dalmatinskiportal.hr/split-je-prostornoplanski-zapusten-duilovo-ide-putem-znjana-suta-otkrio-velike-projekte-koji-ce-mijenjati-grad)
- Put Supavla: the odluka (SGGS 23/23) sets deadlines of 30 days for authorities' requirements, 60 days for the nacrt prijedloga (draft proposal), a 30-day javna rasprava, then 15 + 15 days. That is about 5–6 months nominally. Still "u izradi" in September 2026. — [Odluka SGGS 23/23](https://split.hr/Portals/0/adam/Contents/l9lHOskPX061RyqHuugxXQ/Link/Odluka%20o%20izradi%20Urbanisti%C4%8Dkog%20plana%20ure%C4%91enja%20podru%C4%8Dja%20sjeverozapadno%20od%20kri%C5%BEanja%20ulica%20Put%20Stinica%20i%20Put%20Supavla.pdf); [Planovi u izradi](https://split.hr/ukljuci-se/prostorno-planska-dokumentacija/planovi-u-izradi)
- The ID GUP itself: odluka 2021 (SGGS 44/21). By September 2026 it had had two public consultations and was still not adopted, more than 5 years on. The original estimate was 12–18 months. — [Glas Istre](https://www.glasistre.hr/hrvatska/split-gradsko-vijece-prihvatilo-prijedloge-o-izradi-izmjena-gup-a-i-ppu-a-747510); [split.hr, 30 Oct 2025](https://split.hr/clanak/odrzana-tematska-sjednica-gradskog-vijeca-o-izmjenama-i-dopunama-ppug-a-i-gup-a)
- Plans stavljeni izvan snage (repealed) or being repealed:
  - DPU dijela kompleksa S 4-1 stambenog naselja Mertojak, repealed by SGGS 44/24 — [Arhiva planova](https://split.hr/strateski-dokumenti/prostorno-planska-dokumentacija/arhiva-planova)
  - DPU Obrtno tehničke škole i športskih sadržaja u Špinutu: repeal procedure under way — [Planovi u izradi](https://split.hr/ukljuci-se/prostorno-planska-dokumentacija/planovi-u-izradi)
  - In 2009 the Gradsko vijeće adopted an odluka o izradi to repeal the PUP kompleksa obiteljskih kuća Široki put na Visokoj, a 3.24 ha plan for single-family houses up to Po+P+2 (published in SGGS 7/09, the same issue as Bilice sjever) — [SGGS 7/09 via split.hr](https://split.hr/strateski-dokumenti/prostorno-planska-dokumentacija/planovi-na-snazi/gis-podatci?EntryId=3108)
- Who initiates and pays:
  - City-funded: Put Supavla ("Izrada Plana financirat će se iz Proračuna Grada Splita"); Bilice sjever (City contract with CPA, 2007).
  - Investor-initiated: DPU dijela područja Dračevac, 2004.
  — sources as cited above

### Inferences
- A realistic planning horizon for a new residential UPU in Dračevac or Bilice is 3+ years from odluka to adoption. It could slip further if tied to GUP changes; Bilice sjever was slowed exactly this way in 2007–08.
- The City has recently framed UPUs as needing a competition and "stručne podloge" (expert groundwork) first. Duilovo and Supavla, and the Mayor's October 2025 remarks, show this. Residents could speed things up by bringing a stručna podloga or analysis themselves.

### Gaps
- No cost figures found for any Split UPU. Budget lines would be in the Proračun or in procurement records (EOJN), which I did not search.
