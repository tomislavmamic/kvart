# UPU/DPU coverage of built-up residential areas in Croatian cities (other than Split)

Research date: 23 September 2026. Every fact below carries the date of the source it comes from. "Verified" means I read it in the cited primary document. Where the only source was a web-page summary (WebFetch/WebSearch digest) and I could not see the underlying document, the finding is marked *(page summary)* and should be treated with moderate confidence.

Legal context in brief (other researchers cover law and procedure): a new **Zakon o prostornom uređenju (NN 155/2025)** was adopted 15 Dec 2025 and **entered into force 1 Jan 2026**. Its Art. 106(2) says a UPU is made for (1) parts of building areas that are not built and not equipped with basic infrastructure, (2) **"postojeće i izgrađene dijelove građevinskih područja za koje se planira urbana preobrazba i/ili urbana sanacija"**, (3) registered cultural-historic ensembles, (4) pomorsko dobro, and (5) areas planned for urbana komasacija. Art. 106(4): a UPU is **not obligatory** for categories 1–4 if a wider plan (PPUG/GUP) already sets conditions "s detaljnošću propisanom za UPU". That exemption does **not** cover komasacija areas (Art. 103(3) for PPUG, Art. 105(3) for GUP; law in force per Art. 250). Art. 106(3) says that until the UPU is adopted in categories 2–5, only reconstruction and replacement builds are allowed, under transitional measures. The law also brings back **urbana komasacija** (Art. 136–137), carried out on the basis of a UPU or a new "urbanistički projekt" — [NN 155/2025](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2025_12_155_2315.html). (The old ZPU 153/13, Art. 79, had the same core rule: a UPU is obligatory for neuređeni dijelovi and for izgrađeni dijelovi planned for urbana preobrazba/sanacija — [Izvješće o stanju u prostoru RH 2013–2019](https://sabor.hr/sites/default/files/uploads/sabor/2021-02-25/154403/IZVJ_STANJE_U_PROSTORU_RH_2013-2019.pdf), p. 157.)

## 1. Which cities have a UPU/DPU for essentially every part / every naselje / every built-up area?

### Takeaway
Full or near-full UPU coverage of built-up areas is **not** the norm in Croatian cities. It exists mainly in **small and medium coastal towns in Primorsko-goranska County and Istria**, where "UPU naselja X" plans cover whole settlements: Opatija has a UPU for practically every naselje, and Mali Lošinj's UPU-9 covers most of the town's building area. The large cities (Zagreb, Rijeka, Pula, Zadar, Varaždin) instead rely on a GUP applied directly, with UPUs/DPUs as islands. In Zagreb, UPUs cover only about 8% of the area of its two GUPs.

### Cited Findings

**National picture**
- On 31 Dec 2019 local-level plans of UPU rank (UPU + surviving DPU + PUP) were concentrated on the coast: **Splitsko-dalmatinska 446, Primorsko-goranska 418, Istarska 364, Zadarska 319**. Among the continental counties the most were in Zagrebačka (144) and Međimurska (139). "Bitno veći broj takvih planova donose JLS-i u priobalnim županijama nego na kontinentu." — [Izvješće o stanju u prostoru RH 2013–2019, p. 157, Slika 34](https://sabor.hr/sites/default/files/uploads/sabor/2021-02-25/154403/IZVJ_STANJE_U_PROSTORU_RH_2013-2019.pdf) (report accepted by Sabor, [NN 105/2021](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2021_09_105_1854.html))

### Inferences
- A UPU covering a whole settlement is established practice in **coastal tourist towns**. The high coastal plan counts (446/418/364/319) point the same way. The likely drivers are (a) the ZOP regime, which blocks building in unbuilt parts without a UPU, and (b) the PGŽ county institute's long-standing practice (it publishes a "Uputa za izradu UPU", [zavod.pgz.hr](https://zavod.pgz.hr/documents/uputa_za_izradu_upu_br.2.pdf)). This is an inference; I did not find a source that states the causal link.
- In the **big cities** (Zagreb, Rijeka, Pula, Zadar, Varaždin) coverage is partial. Zagreb: 1,983 ha of UPUs + 86 ha of DPUs against 23,575 ha of GUP area, i.e. about **8.8%** (my calculation from the report's figures). Most UPUs sit on new-build zones.
- Many "whole-neighbourhood" plans still in force in larger cities are **inherited socialist-era PUPs/DPUs** (Šibenik Meterize and Šubićevac, Zadar Smiljevac, several Rijeka DPUs). They are kept alive and amended, not newly created.
- I found **no Croatian city of more than about 50,000 inhabitants** with UPU coverage of all its built-up residential areas. Opatija (about 10,000 inhabitants) and Mali Lošinj are the closest full-coverage examples. This is an inference from the cases checked, not an exhaustive survey.

### Gaps
- I found no national statistic on the **share of građevinsko područje covered by UPUs**. The national report gives counts per county only; Table 21 of the RH report is an image and could not be read.
- I did not check Umag, Rovinj, Krk, Crikvenica, Novi Vinodolski, Trogir, Omiš, Dubrovnik, Čakovec, Koprivnica, Osijek, Velika Gorica, Samobor or Sveta Nedelja in detail (tool-call budget). The ZPUZZ page for Zagreb County plans only points to a WebGIS viewer.
- For Zadar, Rijeka and Pula, the per-plan counts come from page summaries and should be spot-checked. Whether Zadar currently has a GUP in force was not confirmed; the plans-in-force summary mentioned only the PPUG.

## 2. Is there a model of "UPU for a whole existing neighbourhood"? Concrete examples and results

### Takeaway
There are examples of UPUs/PUPs for existing built neighbourhoods, but the **best-documented evaluation is negative**. Kaštela made about 20 "urban sanation" UPUs for its sprawling residential settlements. In 2019 it concluded that their added value was limited, mostly "nešto novih prometnica". It repealed 12 of them and folded their content into a GUP at 1:5,000. Landowners would not give up land for streets, and the city feared it could not afford to buy it. This is the most directly relevant precedent for Dračevac/Bilice.

### Cited Findings

**Kaštela — the key case (UPUs for existing, sprawling residential areas, later repealed)**
- GUP Kaštela III. izmjene i dopune, obrazloženje (dated July 2019): "U postojećoj fizičkoj strukturi Grada Kaštela… dominira niska i raspršena izgradnja, često neplanska, bez adekvatne ulične mreže… Postojeća izgrađenost je uglavnom između 60 i 75%. Od ukupno gotovo **1800 ha** građevinskog područja neizgrađeno je više od **500 ha**… prometnica, uglavnom širine 3–4 m, bez nogostupa." — [Obrazloženje III. ID GUP Kaštela, p. 13](https://kastela.hr/wp-content/uploads/datoteke/dokumenti/prostorni-planovi-grada/generalni-urbanisticki-plan-kastela---iii.-izmjene-i-dopune/tekstualni-dio/obrazlozenje-iii.-izmjena-i-dopuna-generalnog-urbanistickog-plana-kastela.pdf)
- "Jedan od pokušaja rješavanja ovog problema proteklih godina je bio i kroz izradu **20tak urbanističkih planova uređenja s ciljem urbane sanacije i uspostave kvalitetnije ulične mreže** u neizgrađenim dijelovima. Nažalost, **rezultati nisu bili zadovoljavajući kako zbog odbijanja vlasnika zemljišta da participiraju u formiranju prometnih površina tako i zbog realnog straha Grada od nemogućnosti realizacije odnosno otkupa zemljišta** za realizaciju prometnih i drugih površina u javnom korištenju." — same
- "Od ukupno propisanih **35 UPU-a dovršeno je i usvojeno njih 13**, a u različitim fazama izrade je još 14 planova. Analizirajući usvojene urbanističke planove uočljivo je da je njihova **dodana vrijednost ograničena i odnosi se na nešto novih prometnica i eventualno površina za javne sadržaje i zelenilo**… nisu planirane nikakve složenije urbanističke tipologije… Infrastrukturna rješenja… relativno jednostavna." — same
- Their solution: move the UPU content (street network drawn at **1:5,000**, and new land-use zones) into the GUP. That cut the mandatory UPUs to **3** (Airport, Resnik tourist zone, Kaštel Sućurac–Sustipan/Jugovinil) plus 1 already in force. The stated benefits: a simpler system, and "racionaliziraju se troškovi za izradu prostorno planske dokumentacije i omogućava se veća ulaganja u konkretnija **idejna rješenja i idejne projekte, npr. za ciljane dijelove ulične mreže**", with problems solved "cjelovito". — same
- The amendments were adopted by the city council on 1 July 2019, with one councillor against (for unrelated reasons). Plan coordinator Ines Berlengi said difficulties had been noticed in implementing the previous plans. — [Portal.hr](https://www.portal.hr/novosti/gradska-uprava/40954-gradsko-vijece-usvojene-trece-izmjene-i-dopune-kastelanskog-ppu-a-i-gup-a)
- The city website now lists **12 UPUs with "Odluka o stavljanju izvan snage"**. These include settlement plans such as **UPU 5 Kaštel Novi – Kaštel Stari**, UPU 10 Kaštel Lukšić – Kaštel Kambelovac 2, UPU 11 Kaštel Kambelovac – sjever, UPU 12 and 13 Kaštel Kambelovac centar (1 and 2), UPU 15 Kaštel Gomilica sjever, UPU 16 Kaštel Gomilica – Kaštel Sućurac jug, UPU 4 Rudine, 6 Radun, 17 Giričić, 19 Krtine and 26 Brižine II. Only UPU 27 (radna zona Kaštel Lukšić) and UPU 18 (Sustipan) remain. — [Grad Kaštela, UPU](https://kastela.hr/prostorni-planovi-grada/urbanisticki-planovi-uredenja/) *(page summary)*

I did not verify whether these cover the whole old settlement or only its unbuilt margins.

**Urbana komasacija — practically never used**
- "Institut urbane komasacije nije zaživio u praksi (**provedena je samo jedna komasacija od 2004. godine**)", and the mechanism was abolished by the 2013 ZPU. — [Izvješće o stanju u prostoru RH 2013–2019, p. 157 and p. 133](https://sabor.hr/sites/default/files/uploads/sabor/2021-02-25/154403/IZVJ_STANJE_U_PROSTORU_RH_2013-2019.pdf)
- The 2025 ZPU (in force 1 Jan 2026) brings komasacija back and declares it "od interesa za Republiku Hrvatsku", carried out on the basis of a UPU (with parcels set) or an urbanistički projekt. — [NN 155/2025, Art. 136–137](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2025_12_155_2315.html)

### Inferences
- The Kaštela document is a precise warning for Dračevac/Bilice. A UPU for a built-up family-house area that only draws wider streets will stall if owners refuse to give land and the city has no budget to buy it. The residents' proposal will be more credible if it pairs the UPU with an implementation mechanism: owner participation or agreements, a city land-acquisition budget, a komasacija option under the 2025 law, and an infrastructure programme.
- The 2025 law's return of urbana komasacija, which requires a UPU, arguably makes a UPU for a built-up area more useful now than it was in 2013–2025, when the GUP/PPUG could do the same job. This is an inference; there are no implementation results yet.

### Gaps
- I found no documented outcomes (streets built, land obtained, legalisation follow-up) for any Croatian UPU made for an existing residential neighbourhood.
- I did not find Zadar "UPU Bili brig" or "UPU Plovanija" as adopted UPUs. They may not exist; only a PUP Bili brig amendment and infrastructure projects came up.

## 3. Counter-trend: cancelling DPUs/UPUs and relying on GUP/PPUG with direct application

### Takeaway
The trend is real and documented. The 2011–2013 law changes abolished DPUs and let PPUG/GUP set conditions "with UPU-level detail" instead of a UPU. Cities then cut their mandatory-plan lists: Kaštela went from 35 prescribed UPUs to 3 in 2019, Rijeka states that most of its GUP is applied directly, and Zagreb repealed 6 UPUs and 2 DPUs in 2021–2024. But cities are also still starting new UPUs (Zagreb started 7 in 2021–2024), so a UPU for a built-up area is not unrealistic. It needs a clear sanation or transformation rationale.

### Cited Findings
- The 2011 ZPUG amendment was "prvi korak ka ukidanju DPU-a", and the **2013/2014 ZPU abolished the DPU**; its role passed to the UPU. Old DPUs and PUPs count as UPUs. A UPU "nije obvezno za područje za koje su PPUG/O-om odnosno GUP-om propisani uvjeti provedbe zahvata u prostoru s detaljnošću propisanom za UPU". — [Izvješće o stanju u prostoru RH 2013–2019, pp. 146, 157](https://sabor.hr/sites/default/files/uploads/sabor/2021-02-25/154403/IZVJ_STANJE_U_PROSTORU_RH_2013-2019.pdf)
- The same exemption continues in the 2025 law (Art. 106(4)), except for komasacija areas. — [NN 155/2025](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2025_12_155_2315.html)
- **Kaštela (2019)**: from 35 prescribed UPUs (13 adopted, 14 in preparation) down to 3 plus 1 in force. UPU content was moved into the GUP and adopted UPUs were repealed in a parallel procedure. — [Obrazloženje III. ID GUP Kaštela](https://kastela.hr/wp-content/uploads/datoteke/dokumenti/prostorni-planovi-grada/generalni-urbanisticki-plan-kastela---iii.-izmjene-i-dopune/tekstualni-dio/obrazlozenje-iii.-izmjena-i-dopuna-generalnog-urbanistickog-plana-kastela.pdf); [Grad Kaštela UPU list](https://kastela.hr/prostorni-planovi-grada/urbanisticki-planovi-uredenja/)
- **Zagreb (2021–2024)**: 6 UPUs repealed — Groblje Moravče (7/2024), Oporovec – jug (17/2024), Savska–Šarengradska jug and sjever, Savski park istok and zapad (all 11/2024) — totalling 35.03 ha. Cumulatively, 11 UPU repeal decisions and 2 DPU repeal decisions have been made. In the same period 6 UPUs and 2 amendments were adopted, and 7 new UPUs, 1 DPU amendment and 2 UPU amendments were started. — [Izvješće GZ 2021–2024](https://informator.hr/files/832/8320bbe90f825582d990f677374a41cf6f3dcb4f.pdf)
- The four Savska/Savski park UPUs were repealed in order to draw up **new** UPUs under the city's "parametri održivosti". This is a reset, not an abandonment of detailed planning (public consultation 1–15 Dec 2023). — [Baustela.hr](https://baustela.hr/novosti/grad-zagreb-stavlja-izvan-snage-4-problematicna-urbanisticka-plana-uredenja/); [zagreb.hr, UPU stavljanje izvan snage](https://zagreb.hr/urbanisticki-planovi-ure%C4%91enja-upu-stavljanje-izvan/191601)
- The 2016 amendments to Zagreb's GUP removed the obligation to make UPUs for certain areas (e.g. UPU Ciglana, art. 82), and professional bodies criticised inconsistencies in the list of UPU obligations. — [UHA](https://uha.hr/kljucne-primjedbe-strukovnih-udruzenja-i-ustanova-na-izmjene-i-dopune-gup-a-zagreba/) *(search digest; number of abolished obligations not found)*
- **Rijeka**: the GUP "u najvećem dijelu" is applied directly; a separate list of plans is "u postupku stavljanja izvan snage". — [Grad Rijeka](https://www.rijeka.hr/teme-za-gradane/stanovanje-i-gradnja/urbanisticko-planiranje/prostorni-planovi/generalni-urbanisticki-plan-grada-rijeke/) *(page summary)*
- A GUP may set UPU-level conditions and "prijelazne mjere provedbe zahvata do [UPU] donošenja" — [NN 155/2025, GUP article](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2025_12_155_2315.html)

### Inferences
- The realistic question for Split is not whether a UPU for a built-up area is allowed; the 2025 law explicitly lists "postojeće i izgrađene dijelove… urbana preobrazba i/ili urbana sanacija". The question is whether the city will choose a UPU over putting 1:5,000 street-network detail into its GUP, as Kaštela did. Residents should expect the "just put it in the GUP" counter-argument and the Kaštela precedent from the neighbouring town.
- The arguments for a UPU over a GUP are: (a) only a UPU can underpin urbana komasacija under the 2025 law; (b) a UPU sets exact regulation lines and building plots, and it is the stated basis for transferring land for public uses (Art. 213 of the 2025 law lets owners transfer unbuilt land planned for public use to the city); (c) it comes with a public participation process tied to the neighbourhood.

### Gaps
- I found no systematic national count of repealed UPUs/DPUs after 2013.
- I did not locate Rijeka's actual repeal decisions or their number.

## 5. Systematic UPU programmes for informal / illegally built neighbourhoods ("UPU sanacije")

### Takeaway
No Croatian city runs a systematic programme of adopted "UPU sanacije" plans for informal neighbourhoods that I could find. What exists is (a) the HZPR pilot-project programme of 2016–2018, which produced *draft* sanation UPUs for six continental pilot areas, and (b) Kaštela's roughly 20 sanation UPUs, which were repealed. The HZPR work concluded that implementation depends on owners ceding land and on local funding, and that the statutory instruments "nisu prikladni za situacije koje su nastale mimo zakona".

### Cited Findings
- In October 2016 HZPR invited county institutes to run pilot projects for a methodology of "planova sanacije područja nezakonite gradnje". Six took part: Grad Zagreb, and the Zagrebačka, Sisačko-moslavačka, Koprivničko-križevačka, Brodsko-posavska and Osječko-baranjska counties. — [Izvješće RH 2013–2019, p. 185](https://sabor.hr/sites/default/files/uploads/sabor/2021-02-25/154403/IZVJ_STANJE_U_PROSTORU_RH_2013-2019.pdf)
- Pilot areas: **Vrinice – VII. Retkovec (Zagreb)**, **Bapča and Selnica (Velika Gorica)**, **Podolice (Koprivnica)**, **Južno stadionsko naselje and Gradski vrt (Osijek)**, among others. Each project's task included drawing up a "pilot-prijedlog UPU sanacije ili UPU sanacije i preobrazbe" and questioning "prikladnost UPU-a kao alata", with resident surveys and meetings. — [HZPR, Urbana sanacija – zbornik radova, Sept 2018](https://mpgi.gov.hr/UserDocsImages/Zavod/Publikacije/URBANA%20SANACIJA_zbornik_e-knjiga.pdf)
- Vrinice – VII. Retkovec (Zagreb) pilot, listing preconditions for the sanation measures to be implementable: local-government funding; residents' wish to improve the situation; "rješavanje imovinskopravnih odnosa – stanovnici trebaju odvojiti dio svojih parcela i ograda kako bi se osigurala protočnost i minimalni standard prometne mreže, odnosno pristati na prodaju parcele za park, dječje igralište i centar naselja"; "procedura – **zakonom propisani dokumenti nisu prikladni za situacije koje su nastale mimo zakona**"; and resident support from the start, with contractual commitments on property issues. — same zbornik, pp. 200–201
- Bapča/Selnica (Velika Gorica) pilot: about 1.5 km², 156 legalised buildings (80% in Selnica), completed October 2017. Once the airport expansion zone was reduced, the settlements got building areas, "čime su se stekli uvjeti za izradu urbanističkog plana uređenja sanacije ozakonjene izgradnje". — [ZPUZZ pilot project page](https://www.zpuzz.hr/sadrzaj/poslovi-zavoda/izrada-projekata-studija-i-strucnih-podloga/pilot-projekt-izrade-metodologije-sanacije-nezakonite-gradnje-u-naseljima-bapca-i-selnica-na-podrucju-zagrebacke-zupanije/); [HZPR zbornik](https://mpgi.gov.hr/UserDocsImages/Zavod/Publikacije/URBANA%20SANACIJA_zbornik_e-knjiga.pdf)
- The zbornik also includes a paper on "**povijesna neformalna izgradnja u urbanom području Splita**": economic migrants from the Split hinterland bought farmland and built family houses in the 1950s–60s. This is directly relevant background for Split's informal suburbs. — same zbornik, pp. 238–239
- Osijek-Baranja county published its pilot methodology in 2025. — [prostorobz.hr PDF, Nov 2025](https://prostorobz.hr/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Pilot-projekt-metodologija-sanacije-nezakonite-gradnje-na-podrucju-Osjecko-baranjske-zupanije.pdf) (not read in full); Koprivnica-Križevci: [prostorno-kkz.hr](https://www.prostorno-kkz.hr/projekti/zavrseni/urbana-sanacija)
- Legalisation drew **more than 900,000 applications** under ZPNIZ. — [Izvješće RH 2013–2019, p. 146](https://sabor.hr/sites/default/files/uploads/sabor/2021-02-25/154403/IZVJ_STANJE_U_PROSTORU_RH_2013-2019.pdf)
- 30% of legalisation fees go to the JLS: on 31 Dec 2017 this totalled 439.67 million kn nationally, **14.1 million kn for Grad Split** and 70.2 million kn for Zagreb. These are funds that could arguably finance sanation. — same report, p. 191
- The report says ZPUG never properly regulated "institut urbane sanacije (definicija pojma, određivanje obvezne urbane sanacije, posljedica nezakonite gradnje i ozakonjenja)". — same, p. 133
- The Zagreb city report shows **0 "planova sanacije"** adopted in 2021–2024, under indicator 5.4 Urbana sanacija. — [Izvješće GZ 2021–2024](https://informator.hr/files/832/8320bbe90f825582d990f677374a41cf6f3dcb4f.pdf)
- Kozari bok / Kozari putevi (Zagreb): the city improved these informal settlements through **infrastructure projects**, including a wastewater system in stages (4th stage tendered May 2016), roads and a sports centre. I found no evidence of a UPU. — [zagreb.hr, STP Kozari](https://www.zagreb.hr/izvodjenje-radova-na-izgradnji-stp-kozari-bok-koza/90543); [Gradonačelnik u Kozari boku](https://www.zagreb.hr/gradonacelnik-u-kozari-boku-i-kozari-putevima/108410)

### Inferences
- Nationally, sanation of informal neighbourhoods has been done mostly through **infrastructure investment plus GUP rules** (Kozari bok, Kaštela after 2019), not through adopted UPUs. The HZPR pilots stayed at the level of methodology and drafts.
- A Dračevac/Bilice initiative could present itself as the first real application of the "UPU urbane sanacije" category, now explicit in Art. 106(2)(2) of the 2025 law, and cite the HZPR methodology. It should budget for the land question from day one, as both the Kaštela and HZPR findings warn.

### Gaps
- I could not access the Prostor/hrcak article "Pilot projekt izrade metodologije sanacije…" (Prostor 2019; blocked by a bot check) or the ResearchGate paper "Urbana sanacija područja nezakonite gradnje u Hrvatskoj".
- I found no evidence that any HZPR pilot "UPU sanacije" was subsequently adopted.
- Podstrana and Zadar informal-settlement UPUs were not researched.
