Scope: east Split (Dračevac, Mostine, Harakovac, Bilice), with citywide figures for part A. All work done 27 Sep 2026 on the repository's own sheet-rasterisation pipeline plus DGU orthophotos; nothing in the repo was modified. Figures and a building list are saved next to these notes:

Definitions used throughout (they follow the site's own model in `src/lib/gup-grad/rezim.ts`):
- **In-force waiting area** = GUP in force sheet 4.c "obveza izrade UPU" (blue grid) or "obveza izrade DPU" (red hatch) ∩ in-force urban rule 3.x (low-consolidated) ∩ no plan in force. This is where rezim.ts says new building "čeka" (čl. 104–105, SGGS 55/14).
- **Draft waiting area** = April 2025 sheet 4.d fill urbana sanacija, urbana preobrazba or neuređeno ∩ not inside a plan in force (draft čl. 103(1)). Inside a draft blue UPU outline with no fill = "preporuka" (direct GUP building, draft čl. 103(4)).
- **Building** = 3D-model footprint (Grad Split, `Objekti_Split_2025`, same outlines as `Zgrade_3D/ST_3D_2024`) with touching roof parts merged into one block, blocks ≥ 35 m².

## Is "virtually the same area" true? Where exactly do the two regimes differ in east Split?

### Takeaway
For east Split the premise is essentially true: the draft's obligatory UPU outlines coincide with the in-force 4.c UPU-obligation area almost exactly (Jaccard 0.98), 99–100% of the draft's urbana sanacija there lies inside the in-force UPU obligation and inside low-consolidated rule 3.x, and 92% of the draft's "waiting" area was already "waiting" under the GUP in force. Where they differ, the draft is if anything looser: ~21 ha that wait under the GUP in force (mostly business-zoned I/K land in Mostine and Harakovac) become "preporuka" (buildable) in the draft; the draft adds only ~8 ha, on the Karepovac edge. Citywide the two maps differ much more, but not in the sanacija areas.

### Inferences
- The resident is right that the area did not change: in residential Dračevac 2 the in-force obligation and the draft sanacija/neuređeno fills cover the same land (98% of the plan outline; 98.6% of it rule 3.1). The draft does not add a new building-wait area in east Split; under the site's reading of čl. 104–105 the wait already exists in the GUP in force. The difference is legal wording (the draft names sanacija/neuređeno explicitly in čl. 103(1)), not geography.
- "Virtually the same" should not be generalised citywide: the draft drops UPU obligations on ~345 ha of consolidated city and adds a large preobrazba wait on city-project land. But for sanacija the statement holds citywide too (98%).
- The in-force wait depends on the rule 3.x condition; in east Split that condition is met almost everywhere (3.1 in Dračevac 2 and most of Mostine; 3.2 in Harakovac), so the obligation is "live" there, not merely formal.

### Gaps
- Land-use (namjena) percentages come from the site's colour-classified land-use raster, not re-checked against the legend here; "I/K" dominance in Harakovac/Mostine should be verified before it is quoted.
- The map comparison cannot say whether the City actually treats the in-force obligation as a ban (that is a legal/practice question; see Q2 for evidence of permits).
- Plans adopted after the 2025 sheet (if any) are not in the plans-in-force mask; the official register has 40 plans vs 46 on the 2025 sheet (e.g. "DPU dijela područja Dračevac" is missing from the register but present via the sheet).

## Roughly how many buildings appeared in 2015–2026 in the areas that (in force) require a UPU, and in the 2025 sanacija areas? Is "many new buildings" confirmed, and are they inside or outside those areas?

### Takeaway
Some construction is confirmed, but "many" overstates it for the waiting area itself. In the east-Split in-force waiting area (617 buildings today), 23–25 buildings appeared between the 2017 and 2025 orthophotos (≈4% of the stock, ~3 per year) and 32–44 since 2011 (5–7%). About a quarter of those are large business halls or sports structures; in residential Dračevac 2 only 2–3 appeared since 2017 (5–9 since 2011, several of them sheds). At least 6 of the new buildings in the waiting area coincide with building permits issued 2015–2022, so permits were in fact issued there. New construction was relatively denser just outside the waiting area (≤150 m) and in plan-in-force areas.

### Inferences
- "Many new buildings in the last 10 years" is partly supported: construction did not stop in the waiting area (≈3 new buildings a year since 2017 in east Split, some with permits), so the in-force obligation has not operated as an absolute freeze in practice. But relative to the ~600 existing buildings the rate is low (≈4% since 2017). Since 2011 it is roughly half to two-thirds of the rate in the 150 m band just outside the area (5–7% vs 10–12%); since 2017 the two are similar (≈4% vs 5%). In residential Dračevac 2 it is 2–3 buildings since 2017. Much of the new volume inside the area is commercial.
- Buildings that first appear after the 2011 orthophoto cannot use the legalisation route, so each is either permitted or unlawful; the 6 permit matches show at least some were permitted under the GUP in force, which is consistent with the City using an exception route (e.g. the "ili na drugi način utvrđen ovom Odlukom" clause noted in the earlier report) rather than an outright ban. Which route was used cannot be read from maps.
- A rough, low-confidence extrapolation (assuming east-Split rates elsewhere): ~75–100 new buildings since 2011 and ~55–60 since 2017 in the whole in-force waiting area citywide.

### Gaps
- The 3D model's capture date is not stated (metadata only says created Aug 2025; the same outlines are in `ST_3D_2024`); buildings finished after its capture but visible on the 2025/26 orthophoto are missing, so 2021–2025 counts are lower bounds. Demolished buildings are not counted.
- Merged blocks hide new buildings attached to old ones (5 "old + large addition" blocks, 3 of them in the waiting area) and new floors added on old houses (seen repeatedly: flat roofs in 2011 that carry pitched roofs later). Upward extensions are not counted as new buildings.
- Visual dating error: ~25% of the new calls are marked uncertain (small or tree-covered structures); chips at the window edge were blurred and left unclassified (22 blocks). No second observer.
- Relief displacement of tall buildings (up to several metres) and 1–2 m misregistration between orthophoto years affect the automatic score, not the visual labels.
- The permit cross-check covers only the acts the parallel researcher harvested; absence of a permit point does not mean a building is unpermitted. OSM building history was not used (mapping date ≠ construction date).
- The legalisation cut-off (buildings visible on the DGU 2011 orthophoto) is taken from the brief; the law text was not re-fetched here.
