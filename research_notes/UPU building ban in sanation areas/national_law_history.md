# National law on building before an obligatory UPU (Croatia, 1994 – Sept 2026), with focus on built-up areas planned for "urbana sanacija / preobrazba"

Method and scope:
- Unless stated otherwise, every quotation is from the official Narodne novine (NN) text. I downloaded each text and searched it directly:
  - base laws: ZPU 1994; ZPUG 2007; ZPU 2013; ZoG 2013; ZPU and ZoG 2025
  - amendments: 100/04, 90/11, 65/17, 114/18, 39/19 (ZPU and ZoG), 98/19, 67/23
  - Pravilnici: 106/98 and 152/23
- I did not redo the earlier procedural notes (`../UPU plans for Dračevac and Bilice/upu_procedure_law.md`). I only re-checked and extended the provisions that bear on a building freeze.
- Abbreviations:
  - **ZPU 1994** = Zakon o prostornom uređenju NN 30/94 (amended 68/98, 61/00, 32/02, 100/04)
  - **ZPUG** = Zakon o prostornom uređenju i gradnji NN 76/07 (amended 38/09, 55/11, 90/11, 50/12, 55/12)

## Q1. Before 2026, did national law by itself forbid new building where a UPU was obligatory? A dated timeline of (1) when a UPU is obligatory, (2) what may be permitted before it, and (3) how the permit authority must treat the obligation

### Takeaway
Yes, but only for the categories of land where the **law itself** made a UPU obligatory. Such a ban has existed since 1 Oct 2007, and in its present form since **1 Jan 2014**:
- Old ZPU art. 79(1), in the **original 2013 text**, made a UPU obligatory for "neuređeni dijelovi građevinskog područja" and for "izgrađeni dijelovi tih područja planiranih za urbanu preobrazbu **ili urbanu sanaciju**".
- Art. 79(3) then barred any permit for a **new** building there until the UPU was adopted, or until the PPUG/GUP set UPU-level conditions.
- Art. 79(4) allowed reconstruction and like-for-like replacement.
- The permit rules mirrored this: old ZPU art. 146 for the lokacijska dozvola, and ZoG 2013 art. 110(1) pt. 7 for the građevinska dozvola.

"Urbana sanacija" as a UPU trigger, and the statutory ban, were therefore **not** introduced by NN 67/23 or NN 155/25. They date from NN 153/13. Later amendments only tweaked the wording:
- 65/17 added "unutar građevinskog područja" and broadened the sanacija definition.
- 39/19 replaced "akt" with "lokacijska dozvola i građevinska dozvola".

A UPU obligation that a local plan put on an ordinary built area outside these categories never triggered the statutory ban by itself. Only the plan's own provisions then applied.

### Cited Findings

#### A. 1994 – Sept 2007: Zakon o prostornom uređenju NN 30/94 (published 15 Apr 1994, in force 23 Apr 1994); Split's GUP was adopted in 2006 under this law as amended to 100/04
- In the original 1994 text, the obligation to make a detailed plan was left entirely to plans. Art. 23: the PPUG "može utvrditi obvezu izrade detaljnih planova uređenja". Art. 25 says the same for the "Generalni plan uređenja". Art. 28: "Obveza izrade … detaljnog plana uređenja utvrđuje se prostornim planom šireg područja, drugim dokumentima prostornog uređenja, odnosno programom mjera za unapređenje stanja u prostoru." Art. 34 required only that the lokacijska dozvola be issued "na temelju dokumenta prostornog uređenja". I found no statutory interim ban in the 1994 text — [ZPU NN 30/94](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/260702.html)
- The one statutory "no building without a UPU" rule before 2007 was coastal. NN 100/04 (published 20 Jul 2004, in force 28 Jul 2004) added art. 45a, creating the zaštićeno obalno područje (ZOP: islands, a 1,000 m land strip and a 300 m sea strip). It says: "**Unutar zaštićenoga obalnog pojasa ne može se graditi ako nije donesen urbanistički plan uređenja**, osim objekata infrastrukture izvan granica građevinskog područja" ("within the protected coastal belt no building may take place unless a UPU has been adopted"). NN 100/04 art. 38 kept UPU/DPU obligations and boundaries set by earlier programmes of measures — [NN 100/04](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2004_07_100_1897.html)
- The Pravilnik o sadržaju, mjerilima kartografskih prikaza… NN 106/98 set the GUP's content. The text must include a chapter "3.2.6. Područja primjene posebnih mjera uređenja i zaštite" and odredbe "11. Mjere provedbe plana – 11.1. Obveza izrade detaljnih planova uređenja". The graphic part is map "4. Uvjeti za korištenje, uređenje i zaštitu prostora". The Pravilnik contains no "urbana sanacija/preobrazba" categories (the term "sanacij" does not occur) — [Pravilnik NN 106/98](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/1998_08_106_1463.html)

#### B. 1 Oct 2007 – 31 Dec 2013: ZPUG NN 76/07 (published 23 Jul 2007, in force 1 Oct 2007); Split's 2008 GUP amendments fall here
- Definitions (art. 2):
  - pt. 1.3, izgrađeni dio: "izgrađene i uređene građevne čestice i druge površine privedene različitoj namjeni kao i **neizgrađene i neuređene čestice zemljišta površine do 5.000 m²** koje s izgrađenim dijelom građevinskog područja čine prostornu cjelinu"
  - pt. 1.4, neizgrađeni dio: "jedna ili više neposredno povezanih neizgrađenih i neuređenih čestica zemljišta ukupne površine veće od 5.000 m²"
  - Source: [ZPUG NN 76/07](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2007_07_76_2395.html)
- **UPU obligation, art. 75(2), original text:** "Urbanistički plan uređenja donosi se obvezno za neizgrađene dijelove građevinskog područja naselja i neizgrađena izdvojena građevinska područja izvan naselja **te za dijelove tih područja planiranih za urbanu obnovu**" (a UPU is obligatory for unbuilt parts and for parts planned for urban renewal) — [ZPUG NN 76/07](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2007_07_76_2395.html)
- **Amended by NN 90/11** (published 2 Aug 2011, in force 10 Aug 2011), art. 20: "(2) Za neizgrađene **i neuređene** dijelove građevinskog područja naselja i neizgrađena i neuređena izdvojena građevinska područja izvan naselja te za dijelove tih područja planiranih za urbanu obnovu obvezno se donosi urbanistički plan uređenja ili detaljni plan uređenja." — [NN 90/11](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2011_08_90_1923.html)
- **What may be permitted before the plan, art. 125:**
  - (1) "Lokacijska dozvola, rješenje o uvjetima građenja i rješenje o izvedenom stanju na dijelu građevinskog područja za koji je **prema ovom Zakonu** propisana obveza donošenja urbanističkog plana uređenja ili detaljnog plana uređenja **može se izdati samo na temelju tog plana**." (Where the law requires a UPU/DPU, a permit may be issued only on the basis of that plan.)
  - (2) Buildings there only on a serviced plot (road access, sewage, parking).
  - (3) "Stavak 1. i 2. ovoga članka ne odnosi se na izdavanje lokacijske dozvole i rješenja o uvjetima građenja za građenje **zamjenskih građevina** i za **rekonstrukciju postojećih građevina**."
  - Source: [ZPUG NN 76/07](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2007_07_76_2395.html)
- **Art. 126(1):** where the law does *not* require a UPU/DPU, a permit is issued "u skladu s prostornim planom", again only on a serviced plot. Replacement and reconstruction are exempt (126(2)) — [ZPUG NN 76/07](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2007_07_76_2395.html)
- **Temporary freeze by the council, art. 127:**
  - (1) "U svrhu izrade urbanističkog plana uređenja ili detaljnog plana uređenja, čija je obveza izrade propisana prostornim planom šireg područja, predstavničko tijelo … može odlukom o izradi … zabraniti izdavanje lokacijske dozvole … do donošenja tog plana, **ali ne duže od dvije godine**"
  - (2) extendable by at most one year, then barred for five years
  - This is the only explicit statutory time cap on a plan-related freeze that I found. The old ZPU and the new ZPU contain **no** equivalent: the word "zabran" does not appear in NN 153/13, and in NN 155/25 it appears only for risk and protection zones.
  - Source: [ZPUG NN 76/07](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2007_07_76_2395.html)
- ZOP, art. 49(3): "U ZOP-u se u **neizgrađenom** dijelu građevinskog područja može izdati lokacijska dozvola … samo ako je donesen urbanistički plan uređenja". Under ZPUG the coastal rule was limited to the *unbuilt* part — [ZPUG NN 76/07](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2007_07_76_2395.html)
- Transitional, art. 346:
  - (1) plans under earlier laws stay in force until new ones are adopted
  - (2) "Generalni urbanistički planovi ostaju na snazi najdulje deset godina od dana stupanja na snagu ovoga Zakona"
  - The old ZPU art. 198 later superseded this.
  - Source: [ZPUG NN 76/07](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2007_07_76_2395.html)

#### C. 1 Jan 2014 – 31 Dec 2025: old ZPU NN 153/13 (published 18 Dec 2013, in force 1 Jan 2014, art. 205) and amendments
- **Definitions, art. 3(1), original numbering:**
  - pt. 11: "izgrađeni dio građevinskog područja je područje određeno prostornim planom koje je izgrađeno"
  - pt. 20: "neizgrađeni dio … je područje određeno prostornim planom planirano za daljnji razvoj"
  - pt. 21: "**neuređeni dio** građevinskog područja je neizgrađeni dio građevinskog područja određen prostornim planom na kojemu nije izgrađena planirana osnovna infrastruktura"
  - pt. 34: "**urbana preobrazba** je skup planskih mjera i uvjeta kojima se bitno mijenjaju obilježja izgrađenog dijela građevinskog područja promjenom urbane mreže javnih površina, namjene i oblikovanja građevina, i/ili rasporeda, oblika i veličine građevnih čestica"
  - pt. 35: "**urbana sanacija** je skup planskih mjera i uvjeta kojima se poboljšava karakter izgrađenog dijela građevinskog područja i urbane mreže javnih površina devastiranih nezakonitim građenjem"
  - Source: [ZPU NN 153/13](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2013_12_153_3220.html)
- **Duty to plan sanation measures, art. 53:**
  - (2) plans prescribe implementation conditions, guidelines for narrower plans "i mjere za urbanu sanaciju ako su potrebne"
  - (4) "Mjere za urbanu sanaciju propisuju se prostornim planom za područje za koje se tim planom propisuju uvjeti provedbe zahvata u prostoru"
  - (5) "Mjere za urbanu sanaciju propisuju se prostornim planom **za područja na kojima pretežu zgrade ozakonjene na temelju posebnog zakona**"
  - (6) "Područja iz stavka 4. … planiraju se kao posebne zone urbane sanacije"
  - NN 67/23 only renumbered these as 53(5)–(7) by inserting a new para. 2.
  - Sources: [ZPU NN 153/13](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2013_12_153_3220.html); [NN 67/23 art. 11](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2023_06_67_1101.html)
- **GUP content, art. 78:**
  - (1) the GUP "određuje: 1. neizgrađeni dio … te neuređeni dio tih područja; 2. dio građevinskog područja … planiran za urbanu preobrazbu i urbanu sanaciju; 3. obuhvat urbanističkih planova uređenja koji se **prema ovom Zakonu** donose"
  - (2) it prescribes conditions for all interventions outside UPU areas and guidelines for UPUs
  - (3) it "**može** za dijelove građevinskog područja za koje se prema ovom Zakonu obvezno donosi urbanistički plan uređenja propisivati uvjete provedbe zahvata u prostoru s detaljnošću propisanom za urbanistički plan uređenja"
  - Art. 76 is the parallel rule for the PPUG.
  - Source: [ZPU NN 153/13](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2013_12_153_3220.html)
- **The key article, art. 79, original text in force 1 Jan 2014:**
  - "(1) Urbanistički plan uređenja donosi se obvezno za neuređene dijelove građevinskog područja i za izgrađene dijelove tih područja planiranih za urbanu preobrazbu ili urbanu sanaciju."
  - "(2) Donošenje urbanističkog plana uređenja nije obvezno za područje iz stavka 1. ovoga članka za koje su prostornim planom uređenja, odnosno generalnim urbanističkim planom propisani uvjeti provedbe zahvata u prostoru s detaljnošću propisanom za urbanistički plan uređenja."
  - "(3) **Do donošenja urbanističkog plana uređenja na područjima iz stavka 1. ovoga članka, odnosno propisivanja uvjeta provedbe zahvata u prostoru iz stavka 2. ovoga članka ne može se izdati akt za građenje nove građevine.**" (Until the UPU is adopted, or until UPU-level conditions are set in the PPUG/GUP, no permit for a new building may be issued.)
  - "(4) Iznimno od stavka 3. ovoga članka, akt za građenje može se izdati za rekonstrukciju postojeće građevine i za građenje nove građevine na mjestu ili u neposrednoj blizini mjesta prethodno uklonjene postojeće građevine unutar iste građevne čestice, kojom se bitno ne mijenja namjena, izgled, veličina i utjecaj na okoliš dotadašnje građevine."
  - Source: [ZPU NN 153/13](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2013_12_153_3220.html)
- **NN 65/17** (published 7 Jul 2017, in force 15 Jul 2017):
  - Art. 20: "U članku 79. stavku 1. iza riječi: »sanaciju« dodaju se riječi: »unutar građevinskog područja«."
  - Art. 3 redefined urbana sanacija as new pt. 37: "…poboljšava karakter izgrađenih područja **unutar i izvan granica građevinskog područja** devastiranih nezakonitim građenjem **i na drugi način**".
  - It narrowed osnovna infrastruktura (new pt. 23) to "građevina za odvodnju otpadnih voda i prometna površina preko koje se osigurava pristup do građevne čestice".
  - It added to art. 76 "prostore izvan građevinskog područja planirane za urbanu sanaciju".
  - Source: [NN 65/17](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2017_07_65_1494.html)
- **NN 114/18** (published 19 Dec 2018, in force 1 Jan 2019): institutional changes only (the Zavod, deadlines). No change to arts. 3, 79 or 146 — [NN 114/18](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2018_12_114_2218.html)
- **NN 39/19** (published 17 Apr 2019, in force 25 Apr 2019):
  - Art. 19: "U članku 79. stavku 3. riječ: »akt« zamjenjuje se riječima: »lokacijska dozvola i građevinska dozvola«." From then on, 79(3) reads "…ne može se izdati lokacijska dozvola i građevinska dozvola za građenje nove građevine".
  - Art. 1 rewrote art. 3. Neuređeni dio became pt. 24, same wording. Osnovna infrastruktura (pt. 25) became "građevina za odvodnju otpadnih voda **prema mjesnim prilikama određenim prostornim planom** i prometna površina…". Urbana preobrazba became pt. 40 and urbana sanacija pt. 41, both with unchanged 2017 wording.
  - Source: [NN 39/19](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2019_04_39_801.html)
- **NN 98/19** (published 16 Oct 2019, in force 1 Jan 2020): competence changes only ("Ured državne uprave" → "Nadležno upravno tijelo") — [NN 98/19](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2019_10_98_1939.html)
- **NN 67/23** (published 21 Jun 2023, in force 29 Jun 2023):
  - It introduced ePlanovi, "transformacija prostornih planova" (new pt. 44 and ch. 6.6) and "planovi nove generacije" (art. 62(2)).
  - It did **not** amend art. 79. My search of the text for "članku 79" found nothing.
  - Its art. 146 change only removed "posebnim uvjetima i uvjetima priključenja" from pt. 2.
  - So **no amendment between 2014 and 2025 introduced "urbana sanacija" or the ban. Both are in the original 2013 text.**
  - Source: [NN 67/23](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2023_06_67_1101.html)
- **Lokacijska dozvola, art. 146, original text:**
  - "(1) Lokacijska dozvola se izdaje nakon što je u provedenom postupku utvrđeno da: … 3. je donesen urbanistički plan uređenja, ako se dozvola izdaje na području za koje je **ovim Zakonom** propisana obveza njegova donošenja."
  - "(2) Stavak 1. podstavak 3. ovoga članka ne odnosi se na izdavanje lokacijske dozvole za građenje zamjenskih građevina i za rekonstrukciju postojećih građevina."
  - NN 65/17 art. 45 rewrote para. 1, and the UPU condition became pt. 4 with the same "ovim Zakonom propisana obveza" wording.
  - Sources: [ZPU NN 153/13](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2013_12_153_3220.html); [NN 65/17](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2017_07_65_1494.html)
- **Transitional treatment of plans adopted under earlier laws:**
  - Art. 198(1): "Dokumenti prostornog uređenja doneseni na temelju propisa koji su važili prije stupanja na snagu ovoga Zakona ostaju na snazi do donošenja prostornih planova prema ovom Zakonu". Art. 198(6): their amendments "ne moraju biti izrađene u skladu s propisima donesenim na temelju ovoga Zakona".
  - Art. 200(3): old PUPs and DPUs count as UPUs.
  - **Art. 201:**
    - "(1) Jedinice lokalne samouprave dužne su dopuniti prostorne planove uređenja … na način da u njima odrede neuređene dijelove građevinskih područja i izgrađene dijelove tih područja planirane za urbanu preobrazbu u skladu s ovim Zakonom u roku od dvanaest mjeseci od dana stupanja na snagu pravilnika iz članka 56. stavka 3. ovoga Zakona."
    - "(2) Do ispunjenja obveze iz stavka 1. ovoga članka **neuređenim dijelom građevinskog područja smatraju se neizgrađeni dijelovi** građevinskog područja određeni prostornim planovima uređenja … koji su na snazi na dan stupanja na snagu ovoga Zakona."
    - "(3) Do ispunjenja obveze iz stavka 1. … izgrađenim dijelovima građevinskih područja planiranim za urbanu preobrazbu u smislu ovoga Zakona smatraju se **dijelovi građevinskog područja određeni za urbanu obnovu** prostornim planovima koji su na snazi na dan stupanja na snagu ovoga Zakona."
    - Note: there is **no** deeming rule for "urbana sanacija".
  - Art. 197(2): the old Pravilnik NN 106/98 continues to apply to plans made under earlier laws "i njihove izmjene i/ili dopune donesene nakon stupanja na snagu ovoga Zakona".
  - Source: [ZPU NN 153/13](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2013_12_153_3220.html)
- The Pravilnik under art. 56(3) that starts the art. 201(1) 12-month clock is the Pravilnik o prostornim planovima NN 152/23. It is adopted "Na temelju članka 56. stavka 3." and entered into force 1 Jan 2024 — [Pravilnik NN 152/23](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2023_12_152_2228.html)
- **NN 65/17 art. 60 (transitional):** "Do stupanja na snagu prostornih planova koji se donose na temelju Zakona o prostornom uređenju (NN 153/13) i ovoga Zakona, lokacijske dozvole i drugi akti za provedbu prostornih planova donose se na temelju dokumenata prostornog uređenja donesenih na temelju propisa koji su važili prije stupanja na snagu Zakona o prostornom uređenju (NN 153/13)…" — [NN 65/17](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2017_07_65_1494.html)

#### D. 1 Jan 2014 – 31 Dec 2025: Zakon o gradnji NN 153/13 (in force 1 Jan 2014, art. on entry into force)
- **Art. 110(1), original text.** The building authority must issue a građevinska dozvola (for works that need no lokacijska dozvola) once it establishes, among other things:
  - "3. je glavni projekt u pogledu lokacijskih uvjeta izrađen u skladu s uvjetima za provedbu zahvata u prostoru propisanim prostornim planom"
  - "7. **je donesen urbanistički plan uređenja, ako se dozvola izdaje na području za koje je posebnim zakonom propisana obveza njegova donošenja**"
- **(2)** "Stavak 1. podstavak 7. ovoga članka ne odnosi se na izdavanje građevinske dozvole za građenje zamjenskih građevina i za rekonstrukciju postojećih građevina." (3) defines zamjenska građevina.
- **Art. 112:** for building permits, "u pogledu primjene prostornih planova … na odgovarajući se način primjenjuju odredbe kojima je uređeno izdavanje lokacijske dozvole".
- Source: [ZoG NN 153/13](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2013_12_153_3221.html)
- **ZoG NN 39/19 art. 38** (in force 25 Apr 2019) rewrote 110(1)–(2). Pt. 7 kept the same wording. (2) became "Stavak 1. podstavak 7. … ne odnosi se na … 1. rekonstrukciju postojeće građevine 2. građenje nove građevine na mjestu ili u neposrednoj blizini mjesta prethodno uklonjene postojeće građevine unutar iste građevne čestice, kojom se bitno ne mijenja namjena, izgled, veličina i utjecaj na okoliš dotadašnje građevine." — [ZoG NN 39/19](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2019_04_39_802.html)
- The 2025 Upravni sud u Splitu judgment UsI-1956/2024-11 (29 Oct 2025) quotes ZoG art. 110(1) pts. 1–7, including pt. 7, as the standard checklist in a routine family-house permit case. That case was not about a UPU — [zakon.hr](https://www.zakon.hr/c/sudska-praksa/752180/us-i-1956-2024-11---upravni-sud-u-splitu)

### Inferences
- **The answer to the resident's premise is "yes, the law did change", but in 2014, not recently.** Since 1 Jan 2014 a national statute (old ZPU art. 79(3)) has barred permits for new buildings in any built area that the local plan designates as planned for urbana sanacija or preobrazba, until the UPU exists. It has also barred them in any unbuilt/unserviced part. The ban does not depend on the local plan repeating it.
- **What the law does not do is decide which land falls into those categories.** The PPUG/GUP decides that (old ZPU arts. 76(1), 78(1)). An old plan (Split's GUP 2006/2008, consolidated SGGS 55/14) that does not designate an area for sanacija/preobrazba does not trigger 79(3) for that built area. Two deeming rules in art. 201 applied until the plans were supplemented:
  - unbuilt parts of old PPUGs are treated as neuređeni;
  - old "urbana obnova" areas are treated as preobrazba.
  - There was no deeming for sanacija.
- **This explains the resident's observation that houses kept being permitted for 10 years.** Most new buildings in Dračevac/Mostine were in areas that fell in none of these categories: ordinary izgrađeni dio under the GUP in force, or areas already covered by an adopted UPU/DPU. There the permit only had to conform to the GUP (ZoG 110(1) pt. 3). The earlier notes record GUP 2014 čl. 104, which allowed direct building in niskokonsolidirana areas with no prescribed plan.
  - Plots in the PPUG's neizgrađeni dio were, in principle, already frozen since 2014 by art. 79(3) + 201(2), unless the GUP gave UPU-level detail or a UPU/DPU was in force. I did not verify this plot by plot.
- **What would change with the ID GUP** is the map. Map 4.d of the April 2025 draft designates large residential parts of east Split as "područje urbane sanacije". Once adopted, that designation automatically triggers the statutory ban (see Q2 and Q3). The ban is therefore a legal consequence of a new planning choice, not of a new statute.
- The permit-authority rules (ZPU 146; ZoG 110(1) pt. 7) refer to an obligation prescribed "ovim Zakonom" or "posebnim zakonom", i.e. by statute. A UPU obligation the GUP imposes for some other reason (a map 4.c boundary on an ordinary built area) is not a "zakonom propisana obveza". It can bar a permit only through the plan's own conditions (110(1) pt. 3), not through pt. 7.

### Gaps
- I did not read NN 68/98, 61/00 or 32/02 (1994-law amendments; 68/98 apparently introduced the names GUP/UPU), nor the Uredba o uređenju i zaštiti zaštićenog obalnog područja mora (NN 128/04). The pre-2007 picture rests on the 1994 base text and NN 100/04 only.

## Q2. Does NN 155/25 now impose a statutory ban, from when, for which categories, and does it bite on old plans?

### Takeaway
The new ZPU (in force **1 Jan 2026**) and the new ZoG (same date) keep the 2014 logic.
- A UPU "se donosi" for:
  - unbuilt/unserviced parts;
  - existing built parts "za koje se planira urbana preobrazba i/ili urbana sanacija";
  - heritage ensembles;
  - maritime domain;
  - komasacija areas.
- Until the UPU exists, a building permit can be issued only for reconstruction or like-for-like replacement: new ZPU art. 106(3); new ZoG art. 73(1) pt. 6/(2) pt. 7 and (3).
- **New relaxation:** for a *lokacijska* dozvola, new ZPU art. 180(2) pt. 3 also exempts a new building with access to an existing public road and local sewage solutions.

Nothing in the transitional provisions says whether art. 106(3) applies to plans adopted under the old laws. On a literal reading, the ban bites wherever a plan in force designates an area as planned for sanacija/preobrazba, whatever law it was made under. Old plans without such designations are affected mainly through the factual "not built and not serviced" category. I found no MPGI guidance on this.

### Inferences
- **From 1 Jan 2026 there is a statutory ban under NN 155/25**, with the same scope for built areas as the 2014–2025 ban. In any existing built area that a plan in force designates as planned for urbana sanacija or preobrazba, the only works permitted until a UPU (or a GUP with UPU-level detail) exists are:
  - reconstruction;
  - like-for-like replacement;
  - for lokacijska dozvola only, new buildings with existing public-road access (art. 180(2) pt. 3).
  - The ban does not come from art. 106(3) alone. It comes from the permit conditions: new ZPU art. 180(1) pt. 4 and new ZoG art. 73(1) pt. 6/(2) pt. 7, with the exceptions in 73(3).
- **Old plans.** Permit applications filed from 1 Jan 2026 are governed by the new ZPU/ZoG even when the plan applied is the old GUP (SGGS 55/14).
  - Old-plan areas explicitly designated for sanacija/preobrazba (or, arguably, old "urbana obnova" areas) fall within art. 106(2) pt. 2.
  - Ordinary built areas in the old GUP do not, even if the old GUP drew a UPU boundary around them. Pt. 2 requires that sanacija/preobrazba be "planned", which is a plan designation.
  - Pt. 1 ("nisu izgrađeni i opremljeni osnovnom infrastrukturom") is phrased factually, not as a plan designation. Unbuilt, unserviced plots inside an old GUP could therefore be caught by art. 180(1) pt. 4 / ZoG 73 even if the old plan never called them "neuređeni".
  - This is my reading of the text. I found no guidance or case law confirming it.
- **Tension inside the new system.** For a family house (manje složena zgrada, which gets a građevinska dozvola directly), only reconstruction and replacement escape the UPU requirement (ZoG 73(3)). The broader "existing public road" exception in ZPU 180(2) pt. 3 applies only where a lokacijska dozvola is needed. Two readings are possible:
  - the building-permit route is stricter;
  - the ZPU exception should be read into ZoG 73 through the "posebnim zakonom" reference.
  - Worth raising with MPGI or the permit office.
- **"Prijelazne mjere".** Art. 106(3) presupposes a new-type wider plan with "prijelazne mjere" (art. 105(2) pt. 3). An old GUP has none in that sense. For old plans, the operative constraint is the permit-condition rule, not 106(3) itself.

### Gaps

## Q3. Which law governs the content of Split's ID GUP (April 2025 draft)? What do the Pravilnici say about the map categories and interim rules? Is the draft's freeze required by law or the City's own choice?

### Takeaway
- The ID GUP (odluka o izradi SGGS 44/21) is being finished under the **old ZPU**: NN 67/23 art. 59(1) and NN 155/25 art. 236(2). As an amendment of a pre-2014 plan, it follows the old Pravilnik NN 106/98 (old ZPU art. 197(2)). MPGI's 5 Mar 2026 guidance confirms that existing plans were made under NN 106/98 and are converted to the NN 152/23 structure when transformed. The categories on map 4.d (neuređeni dio, urbana sanacija, urbana preobrazba) come from **old ZPU arts. 78(1) and 79(1)**, not from NN 106/98.
- The draft's rule is čl. 103(1): only a UPU may authorise building in those three categories "ako ovim odredbama ili Zakonom nije određeno drugačije". This mirrors old ZPU art. 79(1)–(3) almost word for word. Given the designation, **the freeze on new houses is a statutory consequence**, under both the old ZPU and NN 155/25.
- The **designation** of whole residential districts as "urbana sanacija" is the City's planning choice. So is the choice not to give those areas UPU-level detail in the GUP (79(2) / 106(4)).
- The draft's list of permitted works (čl. 105(5): streets, small infrastructure, public buildings, R2 recreation, recycling yards) is a City choice. It is arguably *more permissive* than the statute in one respect (the law exempts only reconstruction and replacement) and says nothing about houses. Reconstruction and replacement of houses stay possible through the "ili Zakonom" clause.

### Inferences
- **Governing law for the ID GUP's content:** the old ZPU in the version before NN 67/23 (via 67/23 art. 59(1), since the 2021 odluka predates 29 Jun 2023), preserved by NN 155/25 art. 236(2). The plan-content Pravilnik is NN 106/98 (old ZPU art. 197(2)). Pravilnik NN 152/23 is relevant only if and when the plan is transformed.
  - The 2023 and 2024 amendments to the odluka (SGGS 92/23, 11/24) most likely did not restart the procedure. I did not verify their content.
  - The draft's own vocabulary (three categories, map 4.d, "Zakonom") tracks old ZPU arts. 78–79, not NN 155/25 art. 106. For example, it says "neuređeni dijelovi neizgrađenog dijela", not "nisu izgrađeni i opremljeni".
- **Is the freeze required by law?** Two separate questions:
  1. *Must built east-Split neighbourhoods be designated "urbana sanacija"?* Not strictly.
     - Old ZPU art. 53(5) requires "mjere za urbanu sanaciju" where legalised buildings predominate (repeated in new ZPU art. 77(5)).
     - But neither law says such measures must take the form of a UPU. Art. 53(4) lets the plan prescribe sanation measures for areas where the plan itself sets implementation conditions.
     - Old ZPU 79(2) / new ZPU 106(4) let the GUP avoid the UPU obligation, and hence the freeze, by setting UPU-level conditions itself.
     - Designation and the UPU route are City choices.
  2. *Once designated, must new houses wait for the UPU?* **Yes, by statute:**
     - old ZPU art. 79(3) (if the ID GUP is adopted and applied while the old law governs any permit procedure);
     - new ZPU art. 180(1) pt. 4 and new ZoG art. 73(1) pt. 6 for applications from 1 Jan 2026.
     - The draft's silence on houses therefore does not create the ban. The statute does.
- **Reconstruction and replacement of houses:**
  - The draft's čl. 105(5) list does not mention them, but čl. 103(1) defers to "Zakonom".
  - Old ZPU art. 79(4), new ZPU art. 106(3)/180(2) pts. 1–2 and new ZoG art. 73(3) all allow them.
  - So the draft does not go beyond the law against homeowners here. It would, however, be clearer to state this expressly. Čl. 108(1) limits reconstruction of buildings whose *use* conflicts with the plan to existing gabarits.
- **Where the draft may exceed the law in the permissive direction:** neither old ZPU 79(3) nor new ZoG 73 exempts new streets, infrastructure or public buildings from the UPU requirement. Only reconstruction and replacement are exempt, plus the new ZPU 180(2) pt. 3 lokacijska-dozvola exception. The GUP's čl. 105(5) list may therefore be legally shaky unless the GUP gives those interventions UPU-level detail (79(2)/106(4)) or they fall under other special rules (e.g. linear infrastructure via lokacijska dozvola). This is my inference, not tested.

## Q4. Is the City's statement that the new UPUs have "status sanacijskog plana ili plana urbane preobrazbe što je zakonom propisana obveza" correct, and under which article?

### Takeaway
It is **correct as to the consequence and incomplete as to the cause.** Once the GUP designates built areas as planned for urbana sanacija or preobrazba, the law makes a UPU obligatory there:
- old ZPU art. 79(1), in force since 1 Jan 2014; this is the law the ID GUP is made under;
- new ZPU art. 106(2) pt. 2, since 1 Jan 2026.

It also bars new-building permits until the UPU exists: old ZPU 79(3); new ZPU 180(1) pt. 4; ZoG 110(1) pt. 7 / new ZoG 73. But the law obliges the City to plan sanation *measures* for areas dominated by legalised buildings (old ZPU art. 53(5); new ZPU art. 77(5)), not to make *UPUs*. It expressly allows the GUP to set UPU-level conditions instead (79(2); 106(4)). So "zakonom propisana obveza" describes what follows from the City's own designation. The designation itself is not something the law forces on the City.

### Cited Findings
- Old ZPU art. 79(1) (as amended 2017): "Urbanistički plan uređenja donosi se obvezno za neuređene dijelove građevinskog područja i za izgrađene dijelove tih područja planiranih za urbanu preobrazbu ili urbanu sanaciju unutar građevinskog područja." Art. 79(2): not obligatory if the PPUG/GUP sets UPU-level conditions — [ZPU NN 153/13](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2013_12_153_3220.html); [NN 65/17](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2017_07_65_1494.html)
- MPGI's opinion of 21 Mar 2024 to Općina Tinjan (KLASA 360-01/24-02/96, signed by the State Secretary) quotes 79(1) in this amended form. It explains that a PPUG/GUP replaces the UPU only if its odluka o izradi provides for "neposredna provedba" and it contains both text and maps "u mjerilu propisanom za urbanistički plan uređenja". Otherwise, "sukladno odredbi članka 79., stavka 3. Zakona, temeljem takvog prostornog plana općine **ne može se izdati lokacijska dozvola i građevinska dozvola za građenje nove građevine**" — [MPGI mišljenje 21.3.2024](https://mpgi.gov.hr/UserDocsImages/dokumenti/Propisi/Upute_misljenja/2024_3_21.ZPU.79.2.pdf)
- Old ZPU art. 53(5): "Mjere za urbanu sanaciju propisuju se prostornim planom za područja na kojima pretežu zgrade ozakonjene na temelju posebnog zakona." New ZPU art. 77(5) is to the same effect — [ZPU NN 153/13](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2013_12_153_3220.html); [ZPU NN 155/25](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2025_12_155_2315.html)
- The City's reply in the 2024 consultation report (earlier notes, map_4d_findings.md) was addressed to the county environment department's objection to "33 nova UPU-a". It said the new UPUs are sanation or transformation plans "što je zakonom propisana obveza" — [split.hr izvješće o javnoj raspravi](https://split.hr/ukljuci-se/prostorno-planska-dokumentacija/izvjesca-o-javnim-raspravama/izvjesce-o-javnoj-raspravi-o-prijedlogu-izmjena-i-dopuna-generalnog-urbanistickog-plana-splita-i-strateskoj-studiji-o-utjecaju-na-okolis-izmjena-i-dopuna-generalnog-urbanistickog-plana-splita)

### Inferences
- The accurate citation for the City's statement is **old ZPU NN 153/13 art. 79(1) (as amended by NN 65/17), with 79(3)**, for the ID GUP. For plans made from 2026 on, it is NN 155/25 art. 106(2) pt. 2 with art. 180(1) pt. 4 and new ZoG art. 73.
- Residents could fairly respond as follows: the law also offers art. 79(2) / 106(4) (UPU-level detail in the GUP) and art. 53(4)–(6) / 77(5) (sanation measures prescribed directly in the plan). Choosing the UPU route for large, already-built residential areas, with no funding or timetable, is the City's choice, and it triggers a statutory freeze of indefinite length.

## Q5. Case law and MPGI opinions on permits before an obligatory UPU, and on indefinite freezes

### Takeaway
MPGI has consistently read the law, since 2014, as barring new-building permits in statutory UPU areas until the UPU is adopted: FAQ of early 2014, and opinion of 21 Mar 2024. The High Administrative Court (VUS) has annulled plan provisions that made building depend on a future UPU "čije je donošenje neizvjesno", citing legal certainty (Usoz-73/16, 2017). I found no published VUS or Ustavni sud decision squarely on a permit refused under old ZPU art. 79(3) / ZoG art. 110(1) pt. 7 in a sanacija or preobrazba area. Nor did I find one holding that a statutory freeze of indefinite length violates property rights. The ECtHR's *Sporrong and Lönnroth v. Sweden* principle (long building prohibitions without review or compensation can breach A1P1) is the general European benchmark.

### Cited Findings
- **MPGI FAQ, "Općenito", early 2014.** The question: after the new laws came into force, a county office "ne želi više … izdavati dozvole za neizgrađena građevinska područja". MPGI answered: "Upravni odjel dužan je rješavati sve zahtjeve … Isto, kao i do sada, lokacijska i građevinska dozvola **ne mogu se izdati za građenje na područjima za koje je Zakonom o prostornom uređenju propisano obvezno donošenje urbanističkog plana uređenja dok se taj plan ne donese**. Ta područja su, kao i do sada, neuređeni dijelovi građevinskog područja i izgrađeni dijelovi tih područja planirani za urbanu preobrazbu ili urbanu sanaciju, s time što donošenje urbanističkog plana uređenja nije obvezno za navedena područja za koja su … propisani uvjeti … s detaljnošću propisanom za urbanistički plan uređenja." The answer then explains the art. 201 deeming rules (old unbuilt = neuređeni; old "urbana obnova" = preobrazba) — [MPGI česta pitanja](https://mpgi.gov.hr/?id=176&page=1&url=print)
- **MPGI opinion of 21 Mar 2024** (Općina Tinjan): without a UPU or UPU-level detail in the PPUO, art. 79(3) bars lokacijska and građevinska dozvola for new buildings (quoted in Q4) — [MPGI mišljenje 21.3.2024](https://mpgi.gov.hr/UserDocsImages/dokumenti/Propisi/Upute_misljenja/2024_3_21.ZPU.79.2.pdf)
- **VUS, Usoz-73/16-11 of 28 Feb 2017** (NN 46/2017), objective review of the PPUG Supetar (adopted under ZPUG):
  - It annulled provisions allowing buildings with 5+ flats only "u okviru rješenja UPU-a ili DPU-a". The Court held that the provision "uvjetuje mogućnost gradnje određene vrste građevine uz donošenje UPU-a ili DPU-a na način koji je suprotan svrsi i smislu odredaba ZOPUG-a" and makes building "ovisnom o izradi budućeg UPU-a ili DPU-a, **čije je donošenje neizvjesno**".
  - It also held that the provision "ne dovode do jasnog zaključka … kako primijeniti odredbu u vremenskom razdoblju do donošenja UPU-a", which "dovodi … u pitanje načelo pravne sigurnosti kao temeljno načelo pravnog sustava".
  - It noted the PPUG's own transitional clause allowed building before the UPU "unutar … izgrađenih dijelova građevinskih područja … u prostornoj cjelini manjoj od 5000 m²".
  - Source: [VUS Usoz-73/16-11, NN 46/2017](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/full/2017_05_46_1091.html)
- The Upravni sud u Splitu judgment UsI-1956/2024-11 (2 Oct 2025, published 29 Oct 2025) applies ZoG 2013 art. 110(1), including pt. 7, as the checklist for a family-house permit. The case concerned setbacks, not a UPU — [zakon.hr](https://www.zakon.hr/c/sudska-praksa/752180/us-i-1956-2024-11---upravni-sud-u-splitu)
- **ECtHR, *Sporrong and Lönnroth v. Sweden*, 23 Sep 1982:** long-lasting expropriation permits and prohibitions on construction (up to 23 years), with no possibility of review or compensation, violated Art. 1 Protocol 1 ("fair balance", "individual and excessive burden") and Art. 6 § 1 — [echrlawyer.org summary](https://echrlawyer.org/echr-case-law/sporrong-and-lonnroth-v-sweden/); [HUDOC](https://hudoc.echr.coe.int/app/conversion/pdf?library=ECHR&id=001-74927&filename=CEDH.pdf)
- ZPUG art. 127 (2007–2013) capped a council-imposed pre-plan permit ban at 2 years, plus 1 year, then none for 5 years. The old ZPU (2014) and the new ZPU (2026) have no such cap and no such mechanism. Their bans flow automatically from statute (79(3); 180/73) with no time limit — [ZPUG NN 76/07](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2007_07_76_2395.html); [ZPU NN 153/13](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2013_12_153_3220.html); [ZPU NN 155/25](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2025_12_155_2315.html)
- New ZPU art. 208 gives investors a lever if the City does not adopt an obligatory UPU within 3 years of an "iskaz interesa". The lever concerns *basic infrastructure* in unserviced areas, not sanation areas (see earlier notes) — [ZPU NN 155/25](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2025_12_155_2315.html)

### Inferences
- The legal-certainty reasoning in Usoz-73/16 is the closest Croatian authority a resident could invoke against an **open-ended** plan-based freeze.
  - It was about a *plan* provision that went beyond the law (ZPUG).
  - A freeze that follows directly from statute (79(3); 180/73) cannot be attacked in VUS plan review as "contrary to law". It could only be challenged:
    - in a Constitutional Court review of the statute; or
    - through the plan's designation choice (e.g. arguing that designating fully built, legalised neighbourhoods as sanacija without UPU-level GUP detail, funding or a timetable is disproportionate).
- *Sporrong*-type arguments (A1P1 fair balance) become stronger the longer a freeze lasts without a UPU timetable, funding or compensation. The City's own record matters here: UPU Bilice II–Mostine described as holding land "u svojstvu taoca" for 24 years (earlier notes).
