## Q1. Under the GUP in force (55/14), may a house be built, replaced or extended before the UPU, in a rule 3.1 area inside a map 4.c UPU boundary?

### Takeaway
The more defensible reading is **forbidden**. The GUP in force allows a new house, a replacement house, or an extension/raising of a legal house only through the prescribed plan. Until that plan exists, a legal building may only be reconstructed or change use **within its existing dimensions** ("u postojećim gabaritima"). The "allowed" reading rests mainly on the ambiguous phrase "a za ostalo temeljem ovog Plana" and on the unqualified sentence about extensions in low-consolidated areas (s30-16). The drafting history (2006→2008) shows "ostalo" means *areas without a plan obligation*, not *other kinds of works*. Since 1 Jan 2014, national law (ZPU NN 153/13 čl. 79(3)–(4)) has pointed the same way for areas that count as neuređeno, preobrazba or sanacija. The City's own 2025 Obrazloženje reads the GUP in force this way too.

### Cited findings: the provisions (55/14)

**Čl. 105, the obligation and the list of exceptions**
- The obligation [55/14 s79-26]: *"Utvrđuje se obveza izrade urbanističkih i provedbenih dokumenata prostornog uređenja za obuhvate prema kartografskom prikazu br. „4. … 4.c Područja i dijelovi primjene planskih mjera zaštite, Obuhvat detaljnijih planova“, u mjerilu 1:10.000."* (An obligation to make UPUs/DPUs is set for the boundaries on map 4.c.) — [GUP 55/14](https://split.hr/strateski-dokumenti/prostorno-planska-dokumentacija/planovi-na-snazi/gis-podatci?EntryId=6130&Command=Core_Download)
- The exceptions [55/14 s79-29, s80-0…s80-4]: *"Na temelju ovog Plana, do donošenja provedbenih dokumenata prostornog uređenja, omogućava se ishođenje lokacijskih dozvola i drugih odgovarajućih akata za građenje za:"*
  - "dijelove ulične mreže"
  - "građevine i uređaje prometne i komunalne infrastrukture (javna rasvjeta, benzinske postaje, plinske postaje, garaže, parkirališta i sl.)"
  - "rekonstrukciju i sanaciju postojeće obale u postojećim lukama"
  - "javne i društvene građevine (nakon provedbe natječaja ukoliko je isti propisan…)"
  - recreation areas: "R2 površine do 5000 m² i … rekreacijske sadržaje unutar zona drugih namjena"
  
  Gloss: until the plans are adopted, the GUP itself allows permits only for these items. Houses are not on the list. — [GUP 55/14](https://split.hr/strateski-dokumenti/prostorno-planska-dokumentacija/planovi-na-snazi/gis-podatci?EntryId=6130&Command=Core_Download)
- The second exception, for production and business buildings [55/14 s80-5…s80-9]. Conditions:
  - "lokacija se nalazi unutar zone proizvodne ili poslovne namjene"
  - the site is defined by public roads and infrastructure
  - "minimalna površina građevne čestice je 3000 m²"
  - "obvezna je provedba natječaja"
  
  Housing has no equivalent exception. — [GUP 55/14](https://split.hr/strateski-dokumenti/prostorno-planska-dokumentacija/planovi-na-snazi/gis-podatci?EntryId=6130&Command=Core_Download)

**Čl. 104, consolidated vs low-consolidated areas**
- Consolidated areas [55/14 s79-14…s79-17]. The permitted routes are listed without qualification:
  - "izgradnja i uređivanje prostora na temelju važećih DPU-a i UPU-a…"
  - "izgradnja i uređivanje prostora **temeljem ovog Plana**"
  - "…na temelju propisanih provedbenih dokumenata…"
- Low-consolidated areas (niskokonsolidirana) [55/14 s79-18…s79-21]:
  - (1) building "na temelju važećih provedbenih dokumenata"
  - (2) building "na temelju propisanih provedbenih dokumenata prostornog uređenja za ostale dijelove niskokonsolidiranih područja čije su granice obuhvata i uvjeti uređenja utvrđeni ovim Planom"
  - (3) *"izgradnja i uređivanje prostora temeljem ovog Plana, **za područja za koja nije propisana izrada provedbenog dokumenta prostornog uređenja**."*
  
  Gloss: in low-consolidated areas, building directly under the GUP is available only where no plan is prescribed. The deliberate contrast with the consolidated list is the core textual basis for the freeze. — [GUP 55/14](https://split.hr/strateski-dokumenti/prostorno-planska-dokumentacija/planovi-na-snazi/gis-podatci?EntryId=6130&Command=Core_Download)

**Čl. 73, urban rule 3.1**
- The rule is "Sanacija, uređivanje i urbana obnova djelomično izgrađenih prostora mješovite izgradnje". Its M1 special rules [55/14 s50-13] read: *"Omogućava se nova izgradnja, zamjena postojećih građevina i rekonstrukcija postojećih građevina te uređenje javnih prostora uz izradu provedbenog dokumenta prostornog uređenja , ukoliko je ovim odredbama utvrđena obveza izrade provedbenog dokumenta, a za ostalo temeljem ovog Plana uz slijedeće uvjete:"*
  - Gloss: new building, replacement, reconstruction and public spaces go through the plan where these provisions set an obligation to make one, "and for the rest on the basis of this Plan", under the following conditions.
  - The conditions [s50-14…s50-19] are plot-level parameters:
    - "za novu izgradnju slobodnostojećih građevina Ppmin=500 m²; kig=0,3 … E=Po+P+2 …"
    - "ukoliko se nova (jedna) građevna parcela formira između dvije izgrađene parcele Ppmin=300 m²…"
    - "Ppmin=700 m² … E=Po+P+3"
    - semi-detached houses "Ppmin=400 m²"
    - at least 30% green area
  
  — [GUP 55/14](https://split.hr/strateski-dokumenti/prostorno-planska-dokumentacija/planovi-na-snazi/gis-podatci?EntryId=6130&Command=Core_Download)
- The same formula appears in rule 3.1 for business (K) [s51-2]. — [GUP 55/14](https://split.hr/strateski-dokumenti/prostorno-planska-dokumentacija/planovi-na-snazi/gis-podatci?EntryId=6130&Command=Core_Download)
- Contrast, rule 3.2 (čl. 74, "Nova regulacija na pretežito neizgrađenom prostoru") [s53-17]: *"Omogućava se izgradnja ulične mreže, komunalno opremanje i nova izgradnja uz izradu provedbenog dokumenta prostornog uređenja u granicama obuhvata utvrđenim ovim Planom:"*. There is no "za ostalo". — [GUP 55/14](https://split.hr/strateski-dokumenti/prostorno-planska-dokumentacija/planovi-na-snazi/gis-podatci?EntryId=6130&Command=Core_Download)
- Rule 3.1 contains explicit carve-outs that let specific projects go ahead *before* the prescribed plan:
  - T1 tourism [s51-29]: *"Moguća realizacija temeljem ovog Plana, prije donošenja propisanog provedbenog dokumenta prostornog uređenja."*
  - The sports hall at OŠ Stobreč [s52-2]: "…prije donošenja propisanog provedbenog plana prostornog uređenja"
  - Streljana Stobreč [s53-8]: "Realizacija je moguća na temelju natječaja prije donošenja propisanog provedbenog dokumenta"
  
  Elsewhere:
  - The Trstenik hotel P29 [s55-15]: *"građevna čestica hotela se određuje kao dio područja za koji je propisana izrada provedbenog dokumenta … Omogućava se izdavanje akta za gradnju … temeljem ovog Plana, prije donošenja propisanog provedbenog dokumenta"*
  - Infrastructure everywhere [s13-14]: "…na svim površinama … i prije donošenja provedbenih dokumenata"
  
  — [GUP 55/14](https://split.hr/strateski-dokumenti/prostorno-planska-dokumentacija/planovi-na-snazi/gis-podatci?EntryId=6130&Command=Core_Download)

**Čl. 49, "Rekonstrukcija građevina": existing legal buildings**
- [s30-9]: *"Legalne građevine koje su po namjeni u skladu s namjenom utvrđenom u ovom Planu a nalaze se u obuhvatu provedbenih dokumenata prostornog uređenja, rekonstruiraju se u skladu s odredbama iz tih planova. Do odnošenja [sic, = donošenja] provedbenih dokumenata prostornog uređenja moguća je rekonstrukcija i prenamjena legalnih građevina u postojećim gabaritima."*
  - Gloss: legal buildings inside plan boundaries are reconstructed according to those plans. Until the plans are adopted, reconstruction and change of use only within existing dimensions.
  - When the 2008 amendment inserted this sentence it read "Do donošenja detaljnijih planova…" [3/08 s11-2]. The "odnošenja" in 55/14 is a typo.
- [s30-11]: *"Legalne niske i srednje građevine stambene … **izvan obuhvata** provedbenih dokumenata prostornog uređenja … mogu se rekonstruirati (uključujući nadograđivati)…"*. Adding storeys is expressly tied to being *outside* plan boundaries.
- [s30-16]: *"U nisko konsolidiranom području omogućava se dogradnja i nadogradnja postojećih, legalnih građevina prema urbanim pravilima."* (In the low-consolidated area, extension and raising of legal buildings is enabled according to the urban rules.)
- [s30-2]: *"Odstupanja od urbanih pravila provode se isključivo kroz postupak izrade provedbenih dokumenata prostornog uređenja."*

— [GUP 55/14](https://split.hr/strateski-dokumenti/prostorno-planska-dokumentacija/planovi-na-snazi/gis-podatci?EntryId=6130&Command=Core_Download)

**Čl. 49, "Nisko konsolidirana područja – uvjeti za izgrađene dijelove"**
- [s33-21]: *"Za stambene, stambeno poslovne i gospodarske građevine u nisko konsolidiranim područjima, u zonama mješovite namjene M1 i M2 … unutar izgrađenog dijela građevinskog područja naselja uz uvjet da se usklade sa zahtjevima iz posebnih propisa, omogućava se primjena slijedećih uvjeta **kroz izradu propisanog provedbenog dokumenta prostornog uređenja ili na drugi način utvrđen ovom Odlukom**:"*
  - [s33-22…s34-4] The relaxed conditions:
    - "minimalna površina građevne čestice je do 40% manja od propisane"
    - "kig … ukoliko je veći od propisanog, ne smije se povećavati"
    - "E=Po+P+3"
    - "V=13,0 m"
    - "minimalna udaljenost od međe 1,0 m"
    - fewer parking places, against payment of the market price
  - [s34-5]: "Izgrađeni dijelovi građevinskog područja naselja utvrđeni su Prostornim planom uređenja Grada Splita."
  - Gloss: relaxed parameters for existing buildings in built parts, applicable through the prescribed plan "or in another way set by this Decision".
- [s33-4]: all urban-rule indicators "trebaju biti provjereni kroz izradu propisanih provedbenih dokumenata prostornog uređenja, provedbu natječaja ili izradu idejnih projekata za lokacijsku dozvolu".

— [GUP 55/14](https://split.hr/strateski-dokumenti/prostorno-planska-dokumentacija/planovi-na-snazi/gis-podatci?EntryId=6130&Command=Core_Download)

### Cited findings: the strongest case for "forbidden"
1. **Čl. 104 st. 2 alineja 3** limits direct GUP application in low-consolidated areas to "područja za koja nije propisana izrada provedbenog dokumenta". Consolidated areas get "temeljem ovog Plana" with no such limit. A 3.1 area inside a 4.c boundary is low-consolidated and has a prescribed plan, so the "temeljem ovog Plana" route is closed. [55/14 s79-14…s79-21] — [GUP 55/14](https://split.hr/strateski-dokumenti/prostorno-planska-dokumentacija/planovi-na-snazi/gis-podatci?EntryId=6130&Command=Core_Download)
2. **Čl. 105** is a closed list of what may be permitted "do donošenja", and houses are not on it. The list is phrased as a positive enabling rule ("omogućava se … za:"), so everything else waits for the plan (expressio unius). [s79-29…s80-9] — [GUP 55/14](https://split.hr/strateski-dokumenti/prostorno-planska-dokumentacija/planovi-na-snazi/gis-podatci?EntryId=6130&Command=Core_Download)
3. **Rule 3.1 itself** sends new building, replacement and reconstruction "uz izradu provedbenog dokumenta … ukoliko je … utvrđena obveza". Map 4.c creates that obligation (čl. 105 st. 1). [s50-13] — [GUP 55/14](https://split.hr/strateski-dokumenti/prostorno-planska-dokumentacija/planovi-na-snazi/gis-podatci?EntryId=6130&Command=Core_Download)
4. **The carve-outs inside rule 3.1** would be pointless if 3.1 building could already proceed under the GUP before the plan. Examples: T1, the OŠ Stobreč hall, Streljana, and hotel P29 elsewhere. The 2008 amendment that created the "za ostalo" wording added the first three carve-outs at the same time. [3/08 s20-34, s21-0, s21-29; 55/14 s51-29, s52-2, s53-8, s55-15] — [GUP 3/08](https://split.hr/strateski-dokumenti/prostorno-planska-dokumentacija/planovi-na-snazi/gis-podatci?EntryId=3183&Command=Core_Download); [GUP 55/14](https://split.hr/strateski-dokumenti/prostorno-planska-dokumentacija/planovi-na-snazi/gis-podatci?EntryId=6130&Command=Core_Download)
5. **Existing houses:** s30-9 sets the specific transitional rule for legal buildings inside plan boundaries: reconstruction only "u postojećim gabaritima" until the plan. Extensions that add storeys are granted only "izvan obuhvata" (s30-11). "Do donošenja" only makes sense if "obuhvat provedbenih dokumenata" includes prescribed plans not yet adopted. [s30-9, s30-11] — [GUP 55/14](https://split.hr/strateski-dokumenti/prostorno-planska-dokumentacija/planovi-na-snazi/gis-podatci?EntryId=6130&Command=Core_Download)
6. **Drafting history.** The 2008 amendment changed rule 3.1 M1 from "uz izradu UPU-a (u granicama obuhvata utvrđenim ovim Planom)" to "…ukoliko je ovim odredbama utvrđena obveza izrade detaljnijeg plana, a za ostalo temeljem ovog Plana". [3/08 s20-12]
   - The words replaced were the geographic qualifier ("within the boundaries set by this Plan"), so the substitute is also geographic.
   - "Ostalo" therefore means the rest of the 3.1 area, where there is no obligation.
   - It cannot mean other kinds of works, because the sentence already lists new building, replacement, reconstruction and public spaces. Almost nothing else is left.
   
   — [GUP 3/08](https://split.hr/strateski-dokumenti/prostorno-planska-dokumentacija/planovi-na-snazi/gis-podatci?EntryId=3183&Command=Core_Download); [GUP 1/06, s44-19](https://split.hr/strateski-dokumenti/prostorno-planska-dokumentacija/planovi-na-snazi/gis-podatci?EntryId=3181&Command=Core_Download)
7. **The City's own reading.** The April 2025 Obrazloženje says that, under the new ZPU, in some areas "za koja je dosadašnjim GUP-om bila obvezna izrada prostornog plana užeg područja (UPU-a) … više se ne može propisati ta obveza, **tako da gradnja postaje moguća izravno temeljem GUP-a**". Gloss: in areas where the current GUP required a UPU, building *becomes* possible directly under the GUP. The implication is that it is not possible today. (Obrazloženje, p. 2) — [Obrazloženje 2025](https://split.hr/Portals/0/Dokumenti/Prostorno-planska/Izmjene%20i%20dopune%20Generalnog%20urbanisti%C4%8Dkog%20plana%20Splita%20za%20ponovnu%20javnu%20raspravu/3_%20Obrazlozenje.pdf)
8. **Practice evidence: a location information refusing a permit (different rule).** In the 2024 consultation (item 70, pp. 187–188), an owner in a consolidated zone D3/rule 2.7 reported the City's *Lokacijska informacija* of 17 Nov 2016. It said that "GUP-om (članak 70.) predviđena obveza izrade detaljnijeg plana uređenja". "Kako Grad Split nikada nije pristupio izradi UPU-a … nisu bili u mogućnosti ishoditi građevinsku dozvolu."
   - The City replied that building there "je važećim Planom uvjetovana provedbom natječaja i izradom UPU-a". The draft sets indicators "kako bi se omogućila neposredna provedba".
   - This shows the City's permitting practice treats a prescribed-but-missing plan as blocking permits. It is a rule 2.7 zone with its own plan clause, not rule 3.1.
   
   — [Izvješće o javnoj raspravi, 26 Mar 2025](https://split.hr/DesktopModules/EasyDNNNews/DocumentDownload.ashx?portalid=0&moduleid=2192&articleid=20457&documentid=15067)
9. **Parcel-level evidence (location unconfirmed).** In the same report (item 208, p. 285), a designer asked for "ukidanje nemogućnosti donošenja akata o gradnji do donošenja UPU-a" (lifting the impossibility of permits until the UPU) for k.č. 1025, 1024/1 and 1024/2.
   - The City refused. It said 1024/1 and 1024/2 are correctly neuređeno and 1025 is in "izgrađenom građevinskom području koje je označeno kao područje urbane sanacije".
   - The ban being complained about may refer to the draft rather than the text in force. The cadastral municipality is not given.
   
   — [Izvješće o javnoj raspravi, 26 Mar 2025](https://split.hr/DesktopModules/EasyDNNNews/DocumentDownload.ashx?portalid=0&moduleid=2192&articleid=20457&documentid=15067)

### Cited findings: the strongest case for "allowed"
1. **Grammar of rule 3.1 M1.** The sentence can be split as "[new building, replacement, reconstruction] … te uređenje javnih prostora uz izradu provedbenog dokumenta, ukoliko je … utvrđena obveza, a za ostalo temeljem ovog Plana". On this reading only public spaces (streets and squares) need the plan, and individual plots ("ostalo") are built directly under the GUP "uz slijedeće uvjete".
   - The conditions that follow are plot-level and look written for direct permits. See the infill clause: "ukoliko se nova (jedna) građevna parcela formira između dvije izgrađene parcele Ppmin=300 m²".
   - s33-4 contemplates checking indicators "kroz … izradu idejnih projekata za lokacijsku dozvolu".
   
   [s50-13, s50-14, s33-4] — [GUP 55/14](https://split.hr/strateski-dokumenti/prostorno-planska-dokumentacija/planovi-na-snazi/gis-podatci?EntryId=6130&Command=Core_Download)
2. **s30-16 is unqualified.** "U nisko konsolidiranom području omogućava se dogradnja i nadogradnja postojećih, legalnih građevina prema urbanim pravilima". It does not say "outside plan boundaries". One can argue it is lex specialis for low-consolidated areas and prevails over the general in-gabarit limit of s30-9. — [GUP 55/14](https://split.hr/strateski-dokumenti/prostorno-planska-dokumentacija/planovi-na-snazi/gis-podatci?EntryId=6130&Command=Core_Download)
3. **"Uvjeti za izgrađene dijelove" (s33-21)** offers its relaxed conditions "kroz izradu propisanog provedbenog dokumenta … **ili na drugi način utvrđen ovom Odlukom**".
   - The word "propisanog" shows the paragraph has plan-obligation areas in view. The alternative "na drugi način" then arguably opens a non-plan route for existing buildings in the built part of those same areas.
   - The 2006 version tied this to buildings "evidentirane u zračnim snimcima". That points to a sanation device for already-existing structures. [1/06 s30-4]
   
   — [GUP 55/14](https://split.hr/strateski-dokumenti/prostorno-planska-dokumentacija/planovi-na-snazi/gis-podatci?EntryId=6130&Command=Core_Download); [GUP 1/06](https://split.hr/strateski-dokumenti/prostorno-planska-dokumentacija/planovi-na-snazi/gis-podatci?EntryId=3181&Command=Core_Download)
4. **Čl. 105's list can be read as permissive, not exhaustive.** It lists what the GUP positively enables pending plans, while the urban rules (3.1 "za ostalo") separately enable plot building. On this reading nothing in čl. 105 prohibits a house. [s79-29]
5. **National law exception for existing buildings.** ZPU NN 153/13 čl. 79(4) allowed a permit, even where a UPU is statutorily mandatory, "za rekonstrukciju postojeće građevine i za građenje nove građevine na mjestu ili u neposrednoj blizini mjesta prethodno uklonjene postojeće građevine unutar iste građevne čestice…". So, at least for reconstruction and like-for-like replacement, the law did not block works. — [ZPU NN 153/13](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2013_12_153_3220.html)

### Inferences
- **"Forbidden" is the more defensible reading for new houses on empty plots.**
  - Čl. 104 st. 2 is explicit and has no counterpart that opens a route.
  - The 2008 drafting history fixes the meaning of "ostalo" as geographic.
  - The in-rule carve-outs presuppose a default of waiting.
  - The City reads its plan this way in the Obrazloženje, and its 2016 location-information practice matches.
  - The "public spaces only" parse of rule 3.1 leaves "ukoliko je … utvrđena obveza" without a sensible referent. It also contradicts čl. 104.
- **Replacement ("zamjena") is also plan-routed by rule 3.1.** From 1 Jan 2014, national law (ZPU čl. 79(4)) permitted like-for-like replacement and reconstruction *where the statutory ban applied*. Whether an officer could rely on the law's exception when the GUP itself is stricter is a legal question. The law lifts the statutory ban but does not itself grant plan conditions.
- **Extension or raising of a legal house is the genuinely contested case.**
  - s30-9 (only within dimensions until the plan) and s30-11 (raising only outside plan boundaries) point to "forbidden".
  - s30-16 (low-consolidated: dogradnja/nadogradnja "prema urbanim pravilima") points to "allowed", but it refers back to rule 3.1, which routes reconstruction through the plan where one is required.
  - Harmonised, s30-16 applies in low-consolidated areas *without* a plan obligation. Still, a permitting officer could plausibly have read it more broadly.
- **s33-21 does not open a new route by itself.** "Na drugi način utvrđen ovom Odlukom" refers to routes that exist elsewhere in the Decision, namely direct GUP application where no plan is prescribed (čl. 104 st. 2 al. 3). It also includes the in-gabarit reconstruction of s30-9. It gives relaxed parameters, not a permit basis.
- **The resident's observation that many houses were built with permits is compatible with the "forbidden" reading.**
  - Most "new" buildings in these kvarts are likely ozakonjene (legalised) buildings. Legalisation under the 2012 act does not depend on the UPU obligation. The City says legalisation from 2012 onward gave Split about 15,000 "new existing" buildings contrary to the plan (Obrazloženje p. 25).
  - Others may be outside the 4.c boundary, in other urban rules (2.5, 3.3), or in plans in force (DPU radne zone Dračevac, UPU Bilice II–Mostine).
  - Some may be in-gabarit reconstructions or pre-2008 permits.
  - This is inference. No permit register was checked.

### Gaps
- No court judgment or ministry opinion applying 55/14 čl. 73/104/105 to a house in a 3.1/4.c area was found.
- No register of building permits issued in Dračevac/Mostine/Harakovac since 2008 was consulted. So it is unverified whether the City actually refused new-house permits there, or granted some under a "za ostalo"/s30-16 reading.
- Which urban rule (3.1 vs 2.5 vs 3.3) covers each residential street is not verified from the texts. Map 4.b (EntryIds 3178/3197) would settle it.

## Q2. How did the wording evolve 2006 → 2008 → 2014? Did 2008 add the obligation for Dračevac, Mostine and Harakovac?

### Takeaway
The obligation mechanism did **not** begin in 2008. The 2006 original already had čl. 104 (low-consolidated: GUP-direct only where no plan is prescribed), čl. 105 (obligation by map 4.c, plus a short exception list), and a rule 3.1 that required a UPU for *all* M1 building "u granicama obuhvata utvrđenim ovim Planom".

The 2008 amendments were a partial **loosening**:
- They replaced map 4.c.
- They rewrote rule 3.1 so that the plan is needed only "ukoliko je … utvrđena obveza", with GUP-direct building "za ostalo".
- They added an in-gabarit reconstruction allowance for legal buildings pending plans.
- They added a sentence enabling extensions in low-consolidated areas.
- They widened the čl. 105 exception list.

The 2012–2014 amendments changed terminology only on these points. Whether residential Dračevac, Mostine and Harakovac were already inside the 2006 map 4.c is unverified; only the 2008 sheet was checked.

## Q3. What exactly does the April 2025 draft say, and is it stricter or looser than the text in force? Does it cite a law article?

### Takeaway
The draft replaces the consolidated/low-consolidated test (it deletes čl. 104) with a **category test** (new čl. 103(1)). Building "samo temeljem prostornog plana užeg područja" applies in:
- neuređeni dijelovi of unbuilt land
- izgrađeni dijelovi planned for urbana preobrazba
- izgrađeni dijelovi planned for urbana sanacija

These are mapped on 4.d. Everywhere else a prescribed UPU becomes only a "preporuka" (recommendation), and direct GUP building continues (čl. 103(3)–(4)).

For **sanation areas** the draft is **stricter** than the text in force:
- The pre-plan exception list (čl. 105(5)) is narrower. Infrastructure becomes only "manje" (smaller) buildings, and the production/business exception disappears.
- The 2008 in-gabarit transitional allowance for legal buildings disappears, and so does the extension sentence for low-consolidated areas.
- The relaxed "uvjeti za izgrađene dijelove" disappear.
- Reconstruction is re-based on new-build conditions.

The residual escape is "ako ovim odredbama ili Zakonom nije određeno drugačije", which imports the ZPU čl. 79(4) exception for reconstruction and like-for-like replacement. The draft cites "Zakon" generically in čl. 103(1). The Obrazloženje explains the change as required by the ZPU without citing an article number. The ZPU article that matches is NN 153/13 čl. 79(1)–(4).

- The čl. 105 exception list narrows ("manje" infrastructure buildings), and the production/business exception is gone.
- The explicit in-gabarit reconstruction allowance for legal buildings pending plans (55/14 s30-9) is deleted.
- The low-consolidated extension sentence (s30-16) is deleted, closing the "allowed" argument for nadogradnja.
- The "uvjeti za izgrađene dijelove" relaxation is deleted.
- Reconstruction equals new-build conditions (49.b(2)). Where direct application is barred (čl. 103(1)), even reconstruction needs a legal basis. That basis is "Zakonom … određeno drugačije", i.e. ZPU 153/13 čl. 79(4). The GUP no longer supplies it.
- The draft defines the category by facts: "izgrađeni dijelovi … planirani za urbanu sanaciju". Any plot in a green-shaded sanation area on 4.d is caught, whatever its urban rule.

**Looser than 55/14 elsewhere:**
- Prescribed plans outside the three categories become recommendations, with direct GUP building until they exist (čl. 103(3)–(4)). Under 55/14 čl. 104, any prescribed plan in a low-consolidated area blocked direct building.
- For a plot inside a 4.c obligation area that the draft does *not* shade as sanacija, preobrazba or neuređeno, the draft is looser.

**Largely unchanged for a new house on an empty plot inside a 3.1/4.c area now shaded sanacija or neuređeno:** "forbidden" before, and "forbidden" now in explicit terms. The change is mostly in clarity and in the loss of the transitional allowances for existing houses.

**Since when the national-law ban applies.** Since 1 Jan 2014, ZPU čl. 79(3) and the transitional čl. 201(2)–(3) arguably already barred permits for new buildings, independently of the GUP, in:
- the unbuilt parts of the PPUG building area ("neuređeno" by default)
- areas that plans in force designated for "urbana obnova"

GUP rule 3.1 is titled "Sanacija, uređivanje i urbana obnova…". Whether Split's permitting office applied čl. 201(3) to 3.1 areas is unverified. This belongs to the law researcher.

## Q5. Does the Obrazloženje or the consultation report explain why the regime changes, or claim it does not change?

### Takeaway
The City presents the change as **required by the Zakon o prostornom uređenju**. It frames it as a *reduction* of mandatory UPUs to three statutory categories, with a statutory ban on new-building permits in those categories. ### Inferences
**The City's narrative in one line:** the GUP in force already made building wait for prescribed plans; the new law narrows which plans are mandatory; in sanation, preobrazba and neuređeno areas the law itself now forbids permits for new buildings; elsewhere direct building is opened.

**Answer to the resident:**
- **Nothing new has to have happened in the law** for a ban on new houses to exist in these areas.
- The GUP in force (since 2006/2008) routes new building in prescribed-plan parts of low-consolidated areas through the plan (čl. 104–105).
- Since 2014, the ZPU also bars permits for new buildings in areas it makes UPU-mandatory, but it allows reconstruction and like-for-like replacement.
- The 2025 draft would restate the ban explicitly, tie it to the sanation map, and remove the GUP's own transitional allowances for existing houses. So *for existing houses* it is stricter.
- **The houses built over the past decade** are most plausibly legalised buildings, in-gabarit reconstructions, pre-2008 or pre-2014 permits, or plots outside the 4.c boundary or in other rules or plans in force. This is inference; no permit data was checked.

**The City's "unchanged" claim is narrow.** It covers the urban-rule concept, not the transitional regime for existing buildings. The Obrazloženje openly tightens that regime.
