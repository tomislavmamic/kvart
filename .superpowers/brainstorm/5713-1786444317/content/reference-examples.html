Useful references — and what to borrow from each

These are references, not assets to copy. Kvart’s scene would use original geometry derived from its real roads, buildings and terrain.

RollerCoaster Tycoon Classic

The closest reference for the fixed isometric camera and the pleasure of watching many tiny, readable things happen at once.

Borrow: tiny cars and people, clear silhouettes, looping ambient stories.
Official Atari page ↗

Dorfromantik

A strong reference for readable low-poly land, clustered homes, trees and roads without photorealism or visual clutter.

Borrow: calm palette, simplified terrain, recognizable large-scale geography.
Developer page ↗

ISLANDERS

The clearest reference for modern low-poly buildings, terrain depth and a miniature-model feeling at a neighborhood scale.

Borrow: faceted buildings, soft shadows, bold landmark massing.
